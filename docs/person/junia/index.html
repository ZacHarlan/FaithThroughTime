Person · Apostle

Junia

c. AD 1

Christian in Rome, greeted with Andronicus as Paul's kinsman and fellow prisoner, 'of note among the apostles'. Most ancient writers read the name as a woman's, and 'apostle' here may mean one sent out rather than one of the Twelve; readers differ on both points.
