Person · Ruler

Geshem the Arabian

c. 495 BC

Arab leader who with Sanballat and Tobiah mocked the rebuilding of Jerusalem's wall, and with Sanballat tried to lure Nehemiah to a meeting at Ono to harm him. Nehemiah 6:6 calls him Gashmu.
