Person · Judge

Gideon

c. 1300–1236 BC

Judge called from threshing wheat in a winepress to deliver Israel from Midian, he routed a vast host with only three hundred men so the glory would be God's alone. Also named Jerubbaal; a jar inscription bearing that rare name was unearthed at Khirbet al-Rai in the Judean foothills.
