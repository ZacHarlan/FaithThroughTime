Person · Church leader

Linus

c. AD 20

Christian in Rome who sends greetings to Timothy in Paul's last letter. Early church writers (Irenaeus, Eusebius) name a Linus as the first bishop of Rome after the apostles and identify him with this man; that identification is tradition, not stated in Scripture.
