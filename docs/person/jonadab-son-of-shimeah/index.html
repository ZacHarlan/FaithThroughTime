Jonadab son of Shimeah

c. 1060 BC

Nephew of David and a friend of his cousin Amnon, whom he advised to feign illness to get Tamar alone. After Amnon's murder he calmed David by saying only Amnon was dead. In Chronicles his father is called Shimea; a Jonathan son of Shimeah later killed a giant of Gath.
