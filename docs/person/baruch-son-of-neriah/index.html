Person · Other

Baruch son of Neriah

c. 640 BC

Jeremiah's scribe and companion. He wrote down Jeremiah's prophecies from dictation, read them publicly in the temple, witnessed the purchase of the field at Anathoth, and was taken with Jeremiah to Egypt. The LORD gave him a personal word in Jeremiah 45.
