Person · Patriarch

Noah

2948–1998 BC

Righteous man who walked with God in a corrupt generation, building the ark that carried his family and the animals through the flood. Recipient of the first covenant, sealed by the rainbow; flood traditions across ancient Mesopotamia, including the Gilgamesh epic, echo the memory of a great deluge.
