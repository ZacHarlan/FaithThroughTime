Person · Ruler

Tiglath-Pileser III

c. 780–727 BC

Assyrian king who took tribute from Menahem (2 Kgs 15:19), seized Galilee and Gilead from Pekah (2 Kgs 15:29), and answered Ahaz's plea by capturing Damascus (2 Kgs 16:7-9). He began the Assyrian deportations of Israel (2 Kgs 15:29; 1 Chr 5:26).
