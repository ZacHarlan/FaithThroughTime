Person · Ruler

Antiochus IV Epiphanes

c. 215–164 BC

Seleucid king who plundered the temple in Jerusalem, banned Jewish practice, and set up the abomination of desolation on its altar in December 167 BC, provoking the Maccabean revolt (1 Macc 1:10-64; 2 Macc 5-6). Many readers link him to the little horn of Dan 8:9-12 and to Dan 11:21-35, while some traditions also read these passages of a later figure.
