Person · Ancestor

Neri

c. 620 BC

Named in Luke 3:27 as the son of Melchi and father of Shealtiel, the point where Luke's line through Nathan touches the royal line of Matthew. Matthew 1:12 and 1 Chronicles 3:17 make Shealtiel a son of Jehoiachin (Jeconiah), so the lists disagree. Two classic explanations are offered. One treats it as a levirate or adoptive link, Shealtiel becoming heir to Neri's line when Jehoiachin was recorded as childless (Jer 22:30). The other holds that Luke traces Mary's ancestry, in which Neri is her forebear and the name Shealtiel belongs to a separate line or a shared name. This table keeps Matthew's father for Shealtiel and shows Neri as a parent relationship.
