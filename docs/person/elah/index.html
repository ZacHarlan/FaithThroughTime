Person · King

Elah

c. 955–929 BC

King of Israel, son of Baasha, who reigned two years. While he was drinking himself drunk in his steward's house at Tirzah, Zimri, captain of half his chariots, killed him and destroyed the house of Baasha, as the prophet Jehu son of Hanani had foretold (1 Kgs 16:1-14).
