Ziba servant of Saul

dates unknown

Servant of Saul's house who told David about Mephibosheth, Jonathan's son, and was set to farm his land with his fifteen sons and twenty servants. When David fled Absalom he brought supplies and accused Mephibosheth of treason; Mephibosheth later denied it, and David divided the land between them.
