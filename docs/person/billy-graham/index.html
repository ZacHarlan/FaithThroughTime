Person · Church leader

Billy Graham

AD 1918–2018

An American evangelist whose stadium crusades and broadcasts carried a simple gospel invitation to more people than perhaps any preacher in history. A counselor to presidents who insisted on integrated seating at his meetings, he became the face of twentieth-century evangelicalism.
