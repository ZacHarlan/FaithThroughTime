The Rab-shakeh

c. 750 BC

Rab-shakeh is an Assyrian title (chief cupbearer or field commander), not a personal name. Sennacherib sent him from Lachish to Jerusalem, where he mocked Hezekiah's trust in the LORD, spoke in Hebrew to the men on the wall, and demanded surrender.
