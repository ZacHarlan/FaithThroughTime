Person · Apostle

Peter

c. 2 BC – AD 67

A Galilean fisherman whom Jesus called to lead the apostles, remembered for his bold confession, his denial, and his restoration. He preached at Pentecost, opened the door to Gentile believers at Cornelius's house, and by early tradition was martyred in Rome under Nero. His wife is not named: the Gospels mention his mother-in-law (Mark 1:30) and Paul says Cephas took a wife with him on his travels (1 Cor 9:5).
