Person · Ruler

Sheshbazzar

dates unknown

"Prince of Judah" to whom Cyrus entrusted the Temple vessels, and the first governor of the return, who laid the foundation of the house of God. Some identify him with Zerubbabel, others with Shenazar, an uncle of Zerubbabel (1 Chr 3:18); the text does not say.
