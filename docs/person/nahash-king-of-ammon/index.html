Person · King

Nahash king of Ammon

c. 1135–1039 BC

King of the Ammonites who besieged Jabesh-gilead and offered terms of putting out every right eye, until Saul rescued the city. Later he showed David kindness, so that David tried to comfort his son Hanun when Nahash died.
