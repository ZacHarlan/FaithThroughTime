Person · Church leader

Ignatius of Loyola

AD 1491–1556

Spanish soldier turned priest who wrote the Spiritual Exercises and with six companions founded the Society of Jesus, approved by Pope Paul III in 1540. He served as its first Superior General until his death. Catholic tradition honours him as a saint.
