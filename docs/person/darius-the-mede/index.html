Person · Ruler

Darius the Mede

c. 601 BC

Ruler called Darius the Median who received the kingdom of Babylon at about 62 after Belshazzar's death (Dan 5:31). Pressed by his officials, he had Daniel thrown to the lions and then honored Daniel's God when he was delivered (Dan 6). He is not named outside Daniel and his identity is debated.
