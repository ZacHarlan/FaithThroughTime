Person · Other

Alexander the coppersmith

c. AD 15

Man who did Paul much harm and strongly opposed his message, so that Paul warns Timothy about him. He may be the Alexander whom Paul handed over to Satan in 1 Timothy 1:20, but the text does not say; he is also not clearly the Alexander of the Ephesus riot (Acts 19:33).
