Person · Prophet

Elijah

c. 930 BC

Prophet of Gilead who confronted Ahab and the prophets of Baal on Mount Carmel, where fire from heaven vindicated the Lord. Fed by ravens and met by a still small voice at Horeb, he was taken up in a whirlwind, and Malachi promises his return before the great day of the Lord.
