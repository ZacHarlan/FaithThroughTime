Person · Priest

Pashur son of Immer

c. 650 BC

Priest and chief officer of the temple who had Jeremiah beaten and put in the stocks at the Benjamin gate for his prophecy of disaster. Jeremiah renamed him "Magor-missabib" (terror on every side) and foretold his captivity in Babylon.
