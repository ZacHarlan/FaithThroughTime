Person · Church leader

Pope Leo I

c. AD 400–461

Bishop of Rome from 440 to 461. His letter to Flavian, known as the Tome of Leo, set out the teaching of two natures in one person of Christ and shaped the definition of Chalcedon in 451. He is also remembered for persuading Attila to withdraw from Italy in 452.
