Person · Prophet

Isaiah

c. 783–695 BC

Jerusalem prophet who saw the Lord enthroned in the temple and counseled kings Ahaz and Hezekiah through the Assyrian crisis. His visions of Immanuel and the suffering servant shaped messianic hope, and the great Isaiah scroll from Qumran is the best preserved of the Dead Sea Scrolls.
