Benaiah, son of Jehoiada

c. 1070 BC

Warrior from Kabzeel whom David set over his guard, the Cherethites and Pelethites. He took part in anointing Solomon, executed Joab and Shimei's sentence at Solomon's command, and succeeded Joab as head of the army. Not the later priest Jehoiada of Athaliah's day.
