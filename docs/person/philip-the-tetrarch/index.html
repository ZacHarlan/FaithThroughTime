Philip the Tetrarch

c. 22 BC – AD 34

Son of Herod the Great, tetrarch of Ituraea and Trachonitis (Luke 3:1). He rebuilt Caesarea Philippi and Bethsaida, and according to Josephus married Salome, the daughter of Herodias. He should not be confused with the Philip whom Mark 6:17 calls Herodias's first husband; Josephus names that man as another son of Herod the Great.
