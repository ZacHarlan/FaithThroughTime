Judith, wife of Esau

dates unknown

Daughter of Beeri the Hittite, married to Esau when he was forty; she and Bashemath "were a grief of mind unto Isaac and to Rebekah" (Gen 26:34-35). She is not named in the list of Esau's wives in Genesis 36.
