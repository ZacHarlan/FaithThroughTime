Person · Church leader

Thomas Aquinas

c. AD 1225–1274

Dominican friar and one of the most influential medieval theologians, honored in the Catholic Church as a Doctor of the Church. His Summa Theologiae brought Aristotelian philosophy into a systematic account of Christian doctrine.
