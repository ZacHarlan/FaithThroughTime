Shechem, son of Hamor

c. 1755–1733 BC

Hivite prince, son of Hamor. He seized Dinah, then asked to marry her, and he and his father persuaded the men of the city to be circumcised. Simeon and Levi killed the men of the city while they were sore. He is a different person from the city of Shechem.
