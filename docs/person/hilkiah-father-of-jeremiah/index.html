Hilkiah, father of Jeremiah

dates unknown

A priest of Anathoth in the land of Benjamin and father of the prophet Jeremiah. The text does not say he is the same man as Hilkiah the high priest who found the Book of the Law under Josiah, so the two are kept separate here.
