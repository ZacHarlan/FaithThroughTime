Person · Ruler

Sargon II

d. c. 713 BC

King of Assyria named once in Scripture, in Isaiah 20:1, when he sent his commander Tartan against Ashdod and took it; Isaiah walked stripped and barefoot three years as a sign against Egypt and Ethiopia. Ussher takes him to be Sennacherib under another name.
