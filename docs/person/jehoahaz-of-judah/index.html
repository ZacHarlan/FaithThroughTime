Person · King

Jehoahaz of Judah

633 BC

Son of Josiah, made king by the people of the land at 23 after his father fell at Megiddo. He reigned three months before Pharaoh Necho bound him at Riblah and took him to Egypt, where he died (2 Kgs 23:30-34; Jer 22:10-12).
