Person · Prophet

Moses

1571–1451 BC

Deliverer drawn from the Nile who confronted Pharaoh, led Israel out of Egypt through the sea, and received the law at Sinai. Prophet, lawgiver, and mediator of the covenant, he shepherded Israel through forty wilderness years and viewed the promised land from Nebo; the Torah bears his name.
