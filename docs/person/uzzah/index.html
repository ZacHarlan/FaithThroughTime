Person · Other

Uzzah

d. c. 1045 BC

Son of Abinadab, who drove the new cart that carried the ark from his father's house toward Jerusalem. When the oxen stumbled he steadied the ark with his hand, and God struck him dead there; the place was named Perez-uzzah.
