Person · Patriarch

Joseph

1745–1635 BC

Favored son of Jacob, sold into Egypt by jealous brothers, who rose from prison to become Pharaoh's vizier through the interpretation of dreams. His grain policy saved Egypt and his own family from famine, and his forgiveness of his brothers remains Scripture's great portrait of providence over evil intent.
