Person · Priest

Eliashib the high priest

c. 490 BC

High priest in Nehemiah's day. He and his brother priests rebuilt the Sheep Gate, but he was also allied to Tobiah and gave him a chamber in the Temple courts, which Nehemiah threw out. A grandson married a daughter of Sanballat.
