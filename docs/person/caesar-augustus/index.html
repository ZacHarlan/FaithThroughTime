Person · Ruler

Caesar Augustus

63 BC – AD 14

First Roman emperor (27 BC-AD 14). Luke says his decree that all the world should be taxed brought Joseph and Mary to Bethlehem (Luke 2:1-5). Augustus adopted his stepson Tiberius in AD 4 and named him his successor.
