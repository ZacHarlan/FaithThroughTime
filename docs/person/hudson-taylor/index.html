Person · Church leader

Hudson Taylor

AD 1832–1905

English missionary who went to China in 1853 and in 1865 founded the China Inland Mission, which sent workers to the inland provinces. He dressed and lived as Chinese people did and welcomed missionaries from many denominations and unmarried women.
