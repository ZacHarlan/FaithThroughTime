Person · Priest

Jeshua the high priest

c. 570 BC

High priest of the first return from Babylon, son of Jozadak. With Zerubbabel he rebuilt the altar and laid the foundation of the Temple, and Zechariah saw him in a vision standing before the angel of the LORD.
