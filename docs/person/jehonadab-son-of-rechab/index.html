Jehonadab son of Rechab

c. 930 BC

Zealous ally of Jehu who rode with him in his chariot and helped him destroy the worshippers of Baal in Samaria. He is also the ancestor-figure of the Rechabites, who kept his command never to drink wine, build houses or plant vineyards; Jeremiah later held them up as a model of obedience.
