Person · Prophet

Micaiah son of Imlah

c. 950 BC

Prophet of the LORD in the northern kingdom whom King Ahab hated because he never prophesied good about him. Alone against four hundred court prophets, he told Ahab and Jehoshaphat that the battle at Ramoth-gilead would fail, and was put in prison for it.
