Person · Apostle

John the Apostle

c. AD 6–100

A son of Zebedee and member of Jesus' inner circle, traditionally identified as the beloved disciple behind the fourth Gospel. Linked with Ephesus and exile on Patmos, his legacy endures in the earliest known New Testament fragment, a papyrus scrap of his Gospel known as P52. Matthew 27:56 mentions "the mother of Zebedee's children" at the cross; Mark 15:40 names Salome in the same group, and she is commonly identified with her (an inference).
