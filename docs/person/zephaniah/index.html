Person · Prophet

Zephaniah

c. 660–600 BC

Prophet during Josiah's reign who warned of the Day of the Lord. His line is given in Zephaniah 1:1 back to Hizkiah, who may be King Hezekiah; that is likely to some readers but not certain, and this table does not link the two.
