Person · King

Menahem

c. 810–761 BC

King of Israel for ten years who seized the throne by killing Shallum and brutally sacked Tiphsah (2 Kgs 15:14-16). He paid Pul king of Assyria a thousand talents of silver, raised from Israel's wealthy men, to secure his kingdom (2 Kgs 15:19-20).
