Person · Church leader

Irenaeus of Lyon

c. AD 130–202

Bishop of Lyon who, by his own account, heard Polycarp as a young man. His work Against Heresies is foundational for early Christian orthodoxy and appeals to Scripture, the rule of faith and the succession of bishops from the apostles as tests of true teaching.
