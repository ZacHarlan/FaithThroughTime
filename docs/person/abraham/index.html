Person · Patriarch

Abraham

1996–1821 BC

Patriarch called from Ur of the Chaldees to a land God would show him, receiving the covenant promise that all nations would be blessed through his offspring. His faith, tested in the offering of Isaac, made him the father of the faithful for Jews, Christians, and Muslims alike.
