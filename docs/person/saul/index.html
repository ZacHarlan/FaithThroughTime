Person · King

Saul

c. 1135–1055 BC

Israel's first king, a tall Benjaminite anointed by Samuel, who won early victories over Ammon and the Philistines. His impatience and disobedience cost his house the throne, and his tragic decline beside David's rise ended on Mount Gilboa, where he fell in battle.
