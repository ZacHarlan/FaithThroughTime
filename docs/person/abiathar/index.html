Person · Priest

Abiathar

c. 1085 BC

Son of Ahimelech and the one priest of Nob to escape Saul's massacre. He fled to David, served as his priest and, with Zadok, held the priesthood. He backed Adonijah and Solomon removed him to Anathoth, ending the line of Eli's house in the priesthood as God had said at Shiloh.
