Zichri (Zabdi), of the sons of Asaph

c. 625 BC

Levite of the house of Asaph, father of Micah (Micha) and grandfather of Mattaniah, who led the thanksgiving after the return (1 Chr 9:15; Neh 11:17; 12:35, where he is called Zaccur). "Son of Asaph" here names his family, not his father.
