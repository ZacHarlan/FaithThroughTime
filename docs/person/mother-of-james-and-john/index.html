Mother of James and John

c. 20 BC

Wife of Zebedee and mother of the apostles James and John. She asked Jesus that her two sons might sit at his right and left in his kingdom (Matt 20:20-21) and stood among the women at the crucifixion (Matt 27:56). Scripture does not give her name. Mark 15:40 lists a Salome in the same group of women, and many readers identify the two; that is an inference, and some traditions identify her differently. She is not Salome the daughter of Herodias.
