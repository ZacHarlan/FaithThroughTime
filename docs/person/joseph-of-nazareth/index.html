Joseph of Nazareth

c. 25 BC – AD 20

Husband of Mary and legal father of Jesus, a carpenter of Nazareth described as a righteous man (Matt 1:19). Matthew 1:16 calls him the son of Jacob, and this table follows Matthew for his father. Luke 3:23 calls him the son of Heli, so the two lists disagree. Two classic explanations are offered. Africanus (3rd century) held that Jacob and Heli were half-brothers by the same mother, and that when Heli died childless Jacob married the widow, so Joseph was Jacob's son by birth and Heli's by levirate law. Other writers, including many later Western and Eastern commentators, read Luke as tracing Mary's line, with Heli as her father and Joseph as his son-in-law. The tradition of the church does not require a choice between them.
