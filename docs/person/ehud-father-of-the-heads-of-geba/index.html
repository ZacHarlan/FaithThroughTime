Ehud, father of the heads of Geba

dates unknown

Benjamite named in 1 Chronicles 8:6, whose sons were heads of the fathers of the inhabitants of Geba, removed to Manahath. He is not said to be the judge Ehud (Judges 3:15), who is called the son of Gera, so the two are kept apart; Abihud of verse 3 may be a variant of his name.
