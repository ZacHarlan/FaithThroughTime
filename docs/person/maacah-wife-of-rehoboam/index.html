Maacah, wife of Rehoboam

dates unknown

Wife of King Rehoboam and mother of King Abijah of Judah. Scripture calls her Maachah or Michaiah and gives her father as Absalom (or Abishalom) in 1 Kings 15:2 and 2 Chronicles 11:20, but as Uriel of Gibeah in 2 Chronicles 13:2. Readers commonly explain this by "daughter" meaning granddaughter: Uriel her father, Absalom her grandfather through Tamar. The same name is later given to the "mother" of King Asa, whom Asa removed from being queen for making an idol; many take this to be Asas grandmother.
