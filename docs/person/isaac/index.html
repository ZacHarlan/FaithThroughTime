Person · Patriarch

Isaac

1896–1716 BC

Son of promise, born to Abraham and Sarah in their old age and spared on Mount Moriah when God provided a ram in his place. He reopened his father's wells in the Negev and passed the covenant blessing to Jacob, the quiet link between Abraham and the tribes of Israel.
