Person · Church leader

Benedict of Nursia

c. AD 480–547

Founder of Monte Cassino and author of the Rule of St. Benedict, which became the standard rule of Western monasticism. Its balance of prayer, work and study, later summed up in the motto "ora et labora", shaped monastic communities that preserved learning in the early Middle Ages.
