Person · Prophet

Ahijah the Shilonite

c. 1050 BC

Prophet of Shiloh who tore his new cloak into twelve pieces and gave Jeroboam ten, as a sign that God would take ten tribes from Solomon's son. Years later, old and blind, he told Jeroboam's disguised wife that her son would die and that Jeroboam's house would be cut off. Chronicles says he wrote about Solomon.
