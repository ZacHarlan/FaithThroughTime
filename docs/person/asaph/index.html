Person · Prophet

Asaph

c. 1085 BC

Levite of the Gershonite family, son of Berechiah, whom David appointed chief of the singers before the ark. His sons formed one of the three guilds of Temple musicians, and Psalm 50 and Psalms 73-83 carry his name.
