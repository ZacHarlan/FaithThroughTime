Person · Patriarch

Adam

4004–3074 BC

First man, formed from the dust and given life by the breath of God. Placed in Eden to tend and keep the garden, his disobedience brought sin and death into the human story, and Paul later presents him as the counterpart to Christ, the last Adam who brings life.
