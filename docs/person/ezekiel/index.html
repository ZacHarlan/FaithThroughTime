Person · Prophet

Ezekiel

c. 625–570 BC

Priest deported to Babylon who received his prophetic call amid visions of the divine chariot beside the Chebar canal. Through stark symbolic acts he interpreted Jerusalem's fall and foresaw dry bones living and a new temple; cuneiform records of Judean exile settlements corroborate his community's setting.
