Erastus

c. AD 15

Helper of Paul whom he sent with Timothy from Ephesus into Macedonia. A man named Erastus, the city treasurer, sends greetings in Romans from Corinth, and 2 Timothy says Erastus stayed at Corinth. The text does not say that all three are one man, though many readers think so.
