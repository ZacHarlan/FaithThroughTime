Person · Ruler

Xerxes

c. 519–473 BC

Persian king, son of Darius I and Atossa, who invaded Greece (Dan 11:2 on Ussher's reading, Annals §1100). Most scholars identify him with the Ahasuerus of Esther; Ussher, whom this dataset follows, identifies Ahasuerus with his father Darius I.
