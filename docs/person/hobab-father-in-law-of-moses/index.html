Hobab, father-in-law of Moses

dates unknown

Midianite or Kenite called Moses' father-in-law (Judg 4:11); Numbers 10:29 calls him son of Raguel, so he may be Jethro's son or another name for Jethro. Ancestor of the Kenite Heber.
