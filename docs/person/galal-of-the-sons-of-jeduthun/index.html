Galal, of the sons of Jeduthun

c. 535 BC

Levite of the house of Jeduthun, ancestor of Obadiah (Abda) among those who lived in Jerusalem after the return (1 Chr 9:16; Neh 11:17). "Son of Jeduthun" here names his family, not his father.
