Person · Ruler

Alexander the Great

356–323 BC

King of Macedon whose conquest of the Persian empire (334-323 BC) ended Persian rule over Judea and spread Greek language and culture across the east. Daniel's vision of the he-goat whose great horn is the first king of Grecia is traditionally read as pointing to him (Dan 8:5-8, 21; 11:3-4), and 1 Maccabees opens with his conquests (1 Macc 1:1-7).
