Ahikam son of Shaphan

c. 655 BC

Court official, son of Shaphan, sent by Josiah to Huldah the prophetess about the book of the law. Years later his influence kept Jeremiah from being handed over to the mob that wanted him put to death. He was the father of Gedaliah.
