Person · King

Aethelberht of Kent

c. AD 560–616

King of Kent who received Augustine of Canterbury in 597 and was later baptised, the first Anglo-Saxon king to become Christian. His wife Bertha, a Frankish Christian, had already encouraged the faith at his court.
