Person · Apostle

James, son of Zebedee

c. AD 3–44

Son of Zebedee and brother of John, one of the inner three with Peter and John. Herod Agrippa I had him killed with the sword (Acts 12:2), the only one of the Twelve whose martyrdom Scripture records. Matthew 27:56 mentions "the mother of Zebedee's children" at the cross; Mark 15:40 names Salome in the same group, and she is commonly identified with her (an inference).
