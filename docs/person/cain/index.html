Cain

c. 3950–3000 BC

Firstborn of Adam and Eve, a tiller of the soil whose rejected offering festered into the murder of his brother Abel. Marked yet protected by God, he became the Bible's first city builder and an enduring warning that sin crouches at the door yet can be mastered.
