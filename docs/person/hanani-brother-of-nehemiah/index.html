Hanani, brother of Nehemiah

dates unknown

Nehemiahs brother who came to Susa with men from Judah and told him of the broken walls of Jerusalem. Nehemiah later put him in charge of Jerusalem with Hananiah the ruler of the palace. "Brother" may mean close kinsman.
