Person · Prophet

Samuel

c. 1150–1060 BC

Last of the judges and first of the great prophets, dedicated at the Shiloh sanctuary in answer to Hannah's prayer. He anointed both Saul and David, guiding Israel's passage from tribal confederation to monarchy while insisting that obedience outweighs sacrifice.
