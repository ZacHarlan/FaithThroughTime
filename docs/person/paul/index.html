Person · Apostle

Paul

c. AD 5–67

A Pharisee who persecuted the church until an encounter with the risen Jesus made him the apostle to the Gentiles. His missionary journeys and letters shaped Christian theology more than any other early figure, and the Gallio inscription at Delphi helps anchor his career in history.
