Person · Judge

Shamgar

c. 1340–1290 BC

Son of Anath, named after Ehud as one who "also delivered Israel", killing six hundred Philistines with an ox goad (Judg 3:31). The Song of Deborah recalls "the days of Shamgar" as a time when the highways were deserted (Judg 5:6).
