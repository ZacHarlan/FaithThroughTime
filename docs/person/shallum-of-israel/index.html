Person · King

Shallum of Israel

c. 810–772 BC

Usurper who killed Zechariah son of Jeroboam II and reigned over Israel a full month in Samaria, until Menahem son of Gadi came up from Tirzah and killed him (2 Kgs 15:10-15).
