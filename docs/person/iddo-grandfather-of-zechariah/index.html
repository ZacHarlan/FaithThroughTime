Iddo, grandfather of Zechariah

dates unknown

Called "Iddo the prophet" in Zechariah 1:1, grandfather of the prophet Zechariah. Ezra 5:1 and 6:14 call Zechariah "the son of Iddo," as the line is shortened. A priestly family of Iddo came back with Zerubbabel (Neh 12:4, 16), and Zechariah is named as its head, but the text does not state that this Iddo is the same.
