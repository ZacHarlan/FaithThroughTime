Person · Ruler

Rehum the chancellor

c. 490 BC

Royal officer of the province beyond the River, based in Samaria. With Shimshai the scribe he wrote to Artaxerxes against the Jews rebuilding Jerusalem, and when the king ordered the work stopped he enforced it.
