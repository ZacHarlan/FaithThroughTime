Herod Agrippa I

c. 10 BC – AD 44

Grandson of Herod the Great; executed James son of Zebedee and imprisoned Peter. Struck dead by God. Son of Aristobulus IV, a son of Herod the Great, so grandson of Herod the Great; father of Agrippa II, Bernice and Drusilla, and brother of Herodias (Josephus).
