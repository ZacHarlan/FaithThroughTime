Person · Ruler

Cyrus

c. 600–530 BC

Founder of the Persian Empire who took Babylon and permitted Judah's exiles to return and rebuild the temple. Isaiah strikingly names this Gentile king the Lord's anointed, and the Cyrus Cylinder documents his wider policy of restoring displaced peoples and their sanctuaries.
