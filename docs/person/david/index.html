Person · King

David

1085–1015 BC

Shepherd of Bethlehem who felled Goliath and rose to the throne after Saul, uniting the tribes and taking Jerusalem as his capital. Psalmist and covenant king promised an everlasting dynasty, his house is named on the Tel Dan stele, the earliest extrabiblical witness to his royal line.
