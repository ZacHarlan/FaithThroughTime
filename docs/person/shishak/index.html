Person · Ruler

Shishak

dates unknown

King of Egypt who gave refuge to Jeroboam when Solomon sought his life (1 Kgs 11:40) and in the fifth year of Rehoboam came up against Jerusalem with 1,200 chariots and 60,000 horsemen, taking the treasures of the temple and the palace (1 Kgs 14:25-26; 2 Chr 12:2-9).
