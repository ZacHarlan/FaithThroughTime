Person · King

Solomon

1034–975 BC

Son of David famed for God-given wisdom, he built the first temple in Jerusalem and presided over a golden age of trade, building, and proverbs. Monumental gates at Hazor, Megiddo, and Gezer have long been associated with his projects, yet his foreign marriages sowed the kingdom's division.
