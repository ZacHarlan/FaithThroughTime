Person · Queen

Esther

c. 500–420 BC

A Jewish orphan named Hadassah who became queen of Persia in the citadel of Susa and risked her life to expose Haman's plot to annihilate her people. Her courage is celebrated yearly at Purim; the Ahasuerus of her story is commonly identified with Xerxes I of Persian history.
