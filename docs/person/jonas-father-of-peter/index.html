Jonas, father of Peter

c. 30 BC

Father of Simon Peter. Jesus calls Peter "Simon Bar-jona" (Matt 16:17) and "son of Jona" (John 1:42); in John 21:15-17 the KJV reads "son of Jonas". Some manuscripts of John give the name as John. Peter's brother Andrew is presumed to share this father.
