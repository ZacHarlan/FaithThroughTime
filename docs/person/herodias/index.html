Herodias

c. 15 BC – AD 39

Wife of Herod Antipas; orchestrated John the Baptist's execution through her daughter. Her father was Aristobulus IV (Josephus), so she was the sister of Herod Agrippa I and Antipas's niece. Her first husband was another son of Herod the Great (Mark 6:17 calls him Philip; Josephus calls him Herod).
