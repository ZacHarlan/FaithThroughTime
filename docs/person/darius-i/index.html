Person · Ruler

Darius I

c. 550–486 BC

Persian king who confirmed Cyrus's decree and had the records searched, so that work on the Second Temple resumed and was finished in his sixth year (Ezra 5-6). Haggai and Zechariah prophesied in his second year (Hag 1:1; Zech 1:1).
