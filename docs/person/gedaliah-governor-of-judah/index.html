Gedaliah, governor of Judah

c. 630–588 BC

Son of Ahikam and grandson of Shaphan. After Jerusalem fell, Nebuchadnezzar made him governor over the people left in the land, at Mizpah. Jeremiah lived under his protection. He ignored warnings of a plot and was murdered by Ishmael son of Nethaniah, after which the remnant fled to Egypt.
