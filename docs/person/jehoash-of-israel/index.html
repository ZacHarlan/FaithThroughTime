Person · King

Jehoash of Israel

c. 876–825 BC

King of Israel, son of Jehoahaz. He wept over the dying Elisha, struck the ground only three times with the arrows, and so defeated Ben-hadad three times (2 Kgs 13:14-25). He also defeated Amaziah of Judah at Beth-shemesh and broke down part of Jerusalem's wall (2 Kgs 14:8-14).
