Ahimelech the priest of Nob

c. 1115–1060 BC

Priest at Nob, son of Ahitub, who gave the fleeing David the holy bread and the sword of Goliath. Saul had him and the priests of Nob killed, which Doeg the Edomite carried out. His son Abiathar escaped. Of the house of Eli, in the priestly line of Ithamar.
