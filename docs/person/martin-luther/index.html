Person · Reformer

Martin Luther

AD 1483–1546

An Augustinian monk whose Ninety-five Theses against indulgences ignited the Protestant Reformation. At the Diet of Worms he refused to recant unless convinced by Scripture and plain reason, and while hidden at the Wartburg he translated the New Testament into German, reshaping both church and language.
