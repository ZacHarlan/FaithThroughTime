Person · Warrior

Ittai the Gittite

dates unknown

Soldier from Gath who had joined David only recently with six hundred men. When David urged him to turn back during Absalom's revolt, he swore to follow the king in life or death, and David gave him command of a third of the army at the battle in the forest of Ephraim.
