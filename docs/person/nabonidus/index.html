Person · Ruler

Nabonidus

dates unknown

Last king of Babylon, who spent years at Tema in Arabia and left his son Belshazzar in charge in Babylon. He is not named in Daniel, but Belshazzars offer of "the third ruler in the kingdom" (Dan 5:16, 29) is commonly explained by Nabonidus holding the first place and Belshazzar the second.
