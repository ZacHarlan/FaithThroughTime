Maaseiah, of the sons of Jeshua the high priest (Ezra 10:18)

c. 485 BC

Named among those who had married foreign wives in Ezra's day, of the sons (house) of Jeshua the son of Jozadak (Ezra 10:18); not stated to be the high priest's own sons, since his house is meant.
