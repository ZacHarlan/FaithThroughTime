Person · Priest

Eli

1214–1116 BC

High priest at Shiloh who judged Israel forty years and brought up the boy Samuel. His failure to restrain his sons Hophni and Phinehas brought judgment on his house, and he died at 98 when news came that the ark had been taken (1 Sam 1-4).
