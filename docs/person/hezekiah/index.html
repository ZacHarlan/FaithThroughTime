Person · King

Hezekiah

751–698 BC

A reforming king of Judah who purged idolatry and trusted God when Sennacherib's Assyrian army besieged Jerusalem. His water tunnel from the Gihon spring survives, and the Siloam inscription found in it in 1880 records his workmen's feat, corroborating the biblical account.
