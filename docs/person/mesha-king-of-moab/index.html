Person · Ruler

Mesha, king of Moab

dates unknown

King of Moab, a sheep-breeder who paid Israel a tribute of 100,000 lambs and the wool of 100,000 rams until Ahab died, then rebelled. Jehoram of Israel, Jehoshaphat and Edom invaded; besieged in Kir-hareseth, Mesha took his eldest son, who should have reigned after him, and offered him as a burnt offering on the wall (2 Kgs 3:4-27).
