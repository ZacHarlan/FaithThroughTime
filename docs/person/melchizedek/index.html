Melchizedek

c. 1943 BC

The enigmatic king of Salem and priest of God Most High who blessed Abram and received his tithe. Psalm 110 made his order a royal-priestly ideal, the Dead Sea Scrolls cast him as a heavenly deliverer, and Hebrews sees in him the pattern of Christ's eternal priesthood.
