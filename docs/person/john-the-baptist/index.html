Person · Prophet

John the Baptist

5 BC – AD 32

A wilderness prophet who called Israel to repentance and baptism in the Jordan and pointed to Jesus as the coming one. Executed by Herod Antipas, he is also attested by Josephus, who reports that many Jews saw Antipas's later military defeat as divine judgment for the killing.
