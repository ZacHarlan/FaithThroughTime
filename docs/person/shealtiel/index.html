Shealtiel

c. 595 BC

Son of Jehoiachin (Jeconiah) in Matthew 1:12 and 1 Chronicles 3:17, and father of Zerubbabel in Matthew, Luke and Ezra 3:2 (1 Chronicles 3:19 lists Pedaiah as Zerubbabel's father). Luke 3:27 calls him the son of Neri, so the genealogies differ here. Two classic explanations are offered. One holds that Shealtiel was Jehoiachin's son by birth and Neri's heir by levirate or adoptive succession, Jehoiachin being recorded as childless in Jer 22:30. The other holds that Luke follows Mary's line, in which Neri is her ancestor. This table follows Matthew for his father and shows Neri as a parent relationship. He lived in the exile in Babylon.
