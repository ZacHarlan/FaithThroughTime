Person · Ruler

Pharaoh of the Exodus

c. 1545–1491 BC

The unnamed Egyptian king who enslaved Israel and defied Moses' demand for release through ten devastating plagues. His army's destruction in the sea sealed Israel's deliverance; Scripture withholds his name, and scholars still debate which historical pharaoh he was.
