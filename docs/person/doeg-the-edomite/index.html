Doeg the Edomite

dates unknown

Edomite, the chief of Saul's herdsmen, who was at Nob when David received bread and Goliath's sword and told Saul about it. When Saul's guards would not strike the priests, Doeg killed eighty-five of them and destroyed the city of Nob.
