Person · Judge

Othniel

c. 1470–1390 BC

First judge of Israel: Othniel son of Kenaz, "Caleb's younger brother" (Judg 3:9), of Judah, who took Kirjath-sepher and married Caleb's daughter Achsah. The Spirit of the LORD came upon him and he delivered Israel from Cushan-rishathaim king of Mesopotamia (Judg 3:8-11).
