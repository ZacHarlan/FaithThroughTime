Rizpah, concubine of Saul

c. 1090 BC

Daughter of Aiah and concubine of Saul. Abner's taking of her sparked his break with Ish-bosheth. Years later she kept watch over the bodies of her two sons and five of Saul's grandsons through a whole season, driving off birds and beasts, until David had the bones buried.
