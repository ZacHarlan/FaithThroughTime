Person · Church leader

Origen of Alexandria

c. AD 185–253

Prolific scholar and head of the catechetical school in Alexandria, later teaching at Caesarea in Palestine. Pioneered textual criticism and allegorical interpretation of Scripture. His Hexapla set the Hebrew Old Testament beside several Greek versions in parallel columns. Some of his speculative teachings were later condemned, but his influence on Eastern and Western exegesis was immense.
