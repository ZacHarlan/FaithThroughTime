Person · Prophet

Daniel

c. 620–534 BC

A Judean noble exiled to Babylon who rose to high office under Babylonian and Persian rulers while refusing to compromise his devotion to God. His deliverance from the lions' den and his sweeping visions of successive empires shaped Jewish and Christian hope in God's coming kingdom.
