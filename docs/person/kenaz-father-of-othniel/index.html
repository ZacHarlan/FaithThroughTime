Kenaz, father of Othniel

c. 1515 BC

Father of Othniel, the first judge of Israel. The texts call Othniel "the son of Kenaz, Calebs younger brother," and the Hebrew does not make clear whether the "younger brother" is Othniel or Kenaz.
