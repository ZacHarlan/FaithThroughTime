Person · Prophet

Elisha

c. 920–839 BC

Farmer called from the plow to succeed Elijah, receiving a double portion of his spirit. Serving Israel through the reigns of four kings, he healed Naaman's leprosy, multiplied bread and oil, and raised the dead, extending God's mercy to Israelite and foreigner alike.
