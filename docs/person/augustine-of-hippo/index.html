Person · Church leader

Augustine of Hippo

AD 354–430

A North African rhetorician converted in a Milan garden who became bishop of Hippo and the most influential theologian of the Western church. His Confessions pioneered spiritual autobiography, and his teaching on grace, sin, and the City of God shaped Catholic and Protestant thought alike. His mother Monica and father Patricius are known from the Confessions; his son Adeodatus was born in 372.
