Person · Prophet

Jeduthun

c. 1080 BC

Merarite Levite who led the third guild of Temple musicians, prophesying with the harp, and is called the king's seer. Psalms 39, 62 and 77 carry his name. Chronicles lists Ethan the Merarite where Jeduthun appears elsewhere, and the two are commonly identified.
