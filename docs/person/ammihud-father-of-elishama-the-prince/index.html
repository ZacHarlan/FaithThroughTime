Ammihud, father of Elishama the prince

dates unknown

Ephraimite, father of Elishama, the prince of Ephraim at the census in the wilderness (Num 1:10; 2:18; 7:48). The Ammihud of 1 Chr 7:26, Joshua's ancestor, bears the same name; the text does not say they are one man.
