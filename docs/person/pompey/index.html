Person · Ruler

Pompey

106–48 BC

Roman general who took Jerusalem in 63 BC, ending Hasmonean independence and bringing Judea under Rome; he entered the Holy of Holies but left the Temple treasury untouched (Josephus, Antiquities 14.4). He was killed in Egypt after his defeat by Julius Caesar.
