Person · Church leader

Jerome

c. AD 347–420

A learned and irascible monk and biblical scholar who revised the Latin Gospels and Psalms and translated most of the Old Testament into Latin from the Hebrew, settling in Bethlehem around 386. The resulting Vulgate, a composite text that gradually replaced the Old Latin versions, became the Bible of the Western church for about a thousand years.
