Person · Church leader

John Wesley

AD 1703–1791

Anglican clergyman and leader of the Methodist movement, which separated from the Church of England only after his death. His heart was "strangely warmed" at Aldersgate Street (1738). Traditional estimates say he traveled about 250,000 miles and preached over 40,000 sermons. Son of Samuel and Susanna Wesley; his brother Charles wrote many of the movement's hymns.
