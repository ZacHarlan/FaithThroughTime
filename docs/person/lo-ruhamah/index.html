Person · Other

Lo-ruhamah

c. 774 BC

Daughter of Gomer and Hoseas second child. Her name means "not pitied." Hosea 1:6 says Gomer "bare a daughter" without the words "to him," as it says of the first child, but the children are given their names as signs to Hosea and are counted as his.
