Person · Prophet

Oded the prophet

c. 775 BC

Prophet of the LORD in Samaria in the days of Ahaz of Judah. He met the army of Israel returning with Judean captives and rebuked them, and the leaders of Ephraim then clothed, fed and sent the captives home to Jericho. He is a different Oded from the father of Azariah.
