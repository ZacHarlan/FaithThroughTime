Person · Messiah

Jesus

c. 5 BC – AD 33

The central figure of the Christian faith, proclaimed as Messiah and Son of God, whose teaching, crucifixion under Pontius Pilate, and resurrection stand at the heart of the gospel. The Pilate stone from Caesarea and the Caiaphas ossuary attest the very officials named at his trial.
