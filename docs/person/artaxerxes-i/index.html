Person · Ruler

Artaxerxes I

c. 485–424 BC

Persian king, son of Xerxes, who sent Ezra to Jerusalem in his seventh year (Ezra 7) and Nehemiah, his cupbearer, in his twentieth (Neh 2:1-8). He also ordered a halt to the rebuilding of Jerusalem's walls after complaints from its neighbours (Ezra 4:7-23).
