Person · Warrior

Judas Maccabeus

c. 196–161 BC

Third son of the priest Mattathias, who led the Jewish revolt after his father's death in 166 BC, defeated Seleucid armies, and rededicated the temple in December 165 BC (Ussher; standard dating 164 BC), the event commemorated at Hanukkah (1 Macc 3-4; 2 Macc 8-10; John 10:22). He fell in battle at Elasa in 161 BC (1 Macc 9:5-18; Ussher).
