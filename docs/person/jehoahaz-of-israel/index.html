Person · King

Jehoahaz of Israel

c. 895–840 BC

King of Israel, son of Jehu, who reigned seventeen years. Hazael of Aram and his son Ben-hadad oppressed Israel throughout his reign until he besought the Lord, who gave Israel a deliverer (2 Kgs 13:1-9).
