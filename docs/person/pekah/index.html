Person · King

Pekah

c. 780–739 BC

King of Israel, son of Remaliah, who killed Pekahiah and allied with Rezin of Aram against Ahaz of Judah (2 Kgs 16:5; Isa 7:1-9). In his days Tiglath-pileser took Galilee and Gilead and carried their people captive, and Hoshea son of Elah conspired against him and killed him (2 Kgs 15:29-30).
