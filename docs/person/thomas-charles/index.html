Person · Church leader

Thomas Charles

AD 1755–1814

Welsh clergyman and Methodist leader at Bala. His effort to supply Welsh-speaking people with Bibles in 1802 is regarded as the immediate cause for founding the British and Foreign Bible Society in 1804.
