Person · Matriarch

Mary, mother of Jesus

c. 20 BC – AD 48

The young woman of Nazareth chosen to bear Jesus, whose obedient response to the angel's announcement made her the most honored woman in Scripture. Her Magnificat echoes Hannah's song, and she appears from Bethlehem to the cross and among the praying disciples before Pentecost. Luke calls Elisabeth her "cousin" (Luke 1:36), a relative of unspecified degree; the two are not sisters.
