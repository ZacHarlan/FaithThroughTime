Person · Priest

Aaron

1574–1452 BC

Elder brother of Moses, his spokesman before Pharaoh, and Israel's first high priest, ancestor of the priestly line. Though he faltered at the golden calf, his benediction endures; the priestly blessing of Numbers 6 appears on the Ketef Hinnom silver amulets, the oldest known copies of biblical text.
