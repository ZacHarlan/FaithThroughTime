Person · Patriarch

Jesse

c. 1140–1060 BC

A Bethlehemite of the tribe of Judah, grandson of Ruth and Boaz, and father of eight sons, the youngest of whom Samuel anointed as king. Isaiah's promise of a shoot from the stump of Jesse made his name shorthand for the messianic hope Christians see fulfilled in Jesus.
