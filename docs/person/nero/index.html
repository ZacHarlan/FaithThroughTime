Person · Ruler

Nero

AD 37–68

Fifth Roman emperor (AD 54-68). He was the Caesar to whom Paul appealed (Acts 25:11). Tacitus records that he blamed the Christians for the great fire of AD 64 and put many to death; church tradition places the deaths of Peter and Paul in his reign.
