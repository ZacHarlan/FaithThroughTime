Araunah the Jebusite

dates unknown

Jebusite who owned the threshing-floor at Jerusalem where the angel stopped the plague. He offered it to David free, but David paid for it and built an altar there. Chronicles calls him Ornan and says Solomon later built the Temple on that site.
