Person · Church leader

Nestorius

c. AD 386–451

Patriarch of Constantinople from 428 who objected to calling Mary Theotokos. The Council of Ephesus deposed him in 431 and he died in exile in Egypt. The Church of the East, often called Nestorian, did not accept his condemnation as fair, and scholars still debate what he actually taught.
