Person · Priest

Ezra, priest with Zerubbabel (Neh 12:1)

c. 570 BC

Chief of the priests who went up from exile with Zerubbabel and Jeshua; the name may denote a priestly house. He may be of the house of Ezra the scribe but is not said to be the scribe himself.
