Person · Prophet

Hananiah son of Azur

c. 640–597 BC

False prophet from Gibeon who told the people in the temple that Babylon's yoke would be broken within two years. He broke the wooden yoke Jeremiah wore. Jeremiah answered that the LORD had not sent him, and he died that same year.
