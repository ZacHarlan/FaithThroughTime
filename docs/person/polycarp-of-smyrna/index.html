Person · Church leader

Polycarp of Smyrna

c. AD 69–155

Bishop of Smyrna, a leading figure of the generation after the apostles who, according to Irenaeus, had known the Apostle John. At his martyrdom he declared "Eighty-six years I have served Him, and He never did me any injury."
