Person · Church leader

Dietrich Bonhoeffer

AD 1906–1945

German Lutheran pastor and theologian who opposed Nazism and was a leader of the Confessing Church. Arrested in 1943 and hanged at Flossenburg concentration camp on 9 April 1945, weeks before its liberation. Author of The Cost of Discipleship and Life Together.
