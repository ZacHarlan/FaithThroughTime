Person · Church leader

Ambrose of Milan

c. AD 339–397

Roman governor made Bishop of Milan by popular acclaim in 374. His preaching influenced Augustine of Hippo, and he baptised him at Easter 387. Western tradition counts him one of the four Doctors of the Church.
