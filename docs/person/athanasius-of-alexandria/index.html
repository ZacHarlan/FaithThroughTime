Person · Church leader

Athanasius of Alexandria

c. AD 296–373

Bishop of Alexandria who defended Christ's full deity against Arianism through five exiles, holding the line for the Nicene faith. His Easter festal letter contains the earliest known list of exactly the twenty-seven New Testament books, a landmark in the history of the canon.
