Koz, head of a priestly family

dates unknown

Ancestor and head of a priestly family that could not find its register and was put from the priesthood (Ezra 2:61-62; Nehemiah 7:63-64). Meremoth son of Uriah, son of Koz, is of this house (Nehemiah 3:4).
