Heman the singer

c. 1085 BC

Kohathite Levite, called the king's seer, who led one of the three guilds of Temple singers and had fourteen sons and three daughters. He is named as a son of Joel and, in a traditional reading, grandson of the prophet Samuel. Psalm 88 is attributed to Heman the Ezrahite, whom some identify with him.
