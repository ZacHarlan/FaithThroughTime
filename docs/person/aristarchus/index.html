Person · Disciple

Aristarchus

c. AD 15

Macedonian of Thessalonica and companion of Paul. He was seized in the Ephesus riot, travelled with Paul toward Asia, sailed with him on the voyage to Rome, and is called Paul's fellow prisoner and fellow labourer.
