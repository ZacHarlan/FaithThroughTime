Person · Warrior

Joshua son of Nun

c. 1553–1443 BC

Successor of Moses and one of the two faithful spies, he led Israel across the Jordan, took Jericho, and apportioned the land among the tribes. His farewell charge to choose whom you will serve sealed the covenant at Shechem, and a fierce burn layer at Hazor recalls the conquest narrative.
