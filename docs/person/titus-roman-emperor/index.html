Titus, Roman emperor

AD 39–81

Son of Vespasian who commanded the Roman army at the siege of Jerusalem, ending with the burning of the Temple in AD 70. He became emperor in AD 79 and died after two years. Not to be confused with Titus, the companion of Paul.
