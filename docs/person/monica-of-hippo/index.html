Monica of Hippo

c. AD 331–387

Christian mother of Augustine of Hippo, who prayed and wept for his conversion for many years and followed him to Milan. She died at Ostia in 387 on the journey home to Africa, shortly after the conversation with her son recorded in the Confessions (book 9). Honored as a saint in the Catholic and Orthodox churches.
