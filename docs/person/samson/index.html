Person · Judge

Samson

1155–1117 BC

Nazirite judge endowed with extraordinary strength, he battled the Philistines single-handedly until Delilah's betrayal cost him his hair, his eyes, and his freedom. In death he toppled Dagon's temple, and his story mirrors Israel's own struggle with compromised devotion.
