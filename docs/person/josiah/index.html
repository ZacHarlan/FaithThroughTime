Person · King

Josiah

649–610 BC

Judah's young king whose temple repairs uncovered the book of the law, igniting the kingdom's most sweeping religious reform. He centralized worship in Jerusalem, renewed the covenant, and kept a Passover unmatched since the judges; scholars link his lawbook to Deuteronomy.
