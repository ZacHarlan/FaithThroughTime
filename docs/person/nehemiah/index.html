Person · Ruler

Nehemiah

c. 485–400 BC

Cupbearer to the Persian king Artaxerxes who returned as governor of Judah and rebuilt Jerusalem's shattered walls in fifty-two days despite fierce opposition. His memoirs join practical leadership to constant prayer, and with Ezra he anchored the restored community in covenant obedience. His brother Hanani brought him news of Jerusalem (Neh 1:2).
