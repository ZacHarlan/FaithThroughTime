Seraiah, ancestor of Ezra

dates unknown

First name in the line of Ezra the scribe in Ezra 7:1, which runs back through Azariah, Hilkiah, Shallum and Zadok to Phinehas and Aaron the chief priest. The list is abridged (compare 1 Chronicles 6:3-15), and "son of" in it means descendant. Most readers identify this Seraiah with Seraiah the chief priest killed at Riblah in 588 BC (2 Kings 25:18; 1 Chronicles 6:14), who is Ezra's ancestor, not his father. Ezra's own father is not named, so no parent link is recorded for Ezra.
