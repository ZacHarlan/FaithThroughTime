Person · Other

Abel

c. 3948–3880 BC

Second son of Adam and Eve, a keeper of flocks whose offering God accepted, provoking his brother's lethal envy. The first innocent to die in Scripture, he is honored in Hebrews as a man of faith whose blood still speaks, and Jesus names him first among the righteous slain.
