Person · Matriarch

Rahab

c. 1475–1400 BC

A woman of Jericho who hid Israel's spies and confessed their God as Lord of heaven and earth, securing her family's rescue by a scarlet cord. Grafted into Israel, she appears in Matthew's genealogy of Jesus and is praised for her faith in both Hebrews and James.
