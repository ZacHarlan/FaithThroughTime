Person · Other

Sheba son of Bichri

d. c. 1023 BC

Benjamite troublemaker who blew a trumpet after Absalom's revolt and called Israel to leave David. Joab chased him to Abel of Beth-maachah, where the townspeople cut off his head and threw it to Joab, ending the rising.
