Helena, mother of Constantine

c. AD 250–330

Mother of Constantine the Great, given the title Augusta by her son. Eusebius reports her pilgrimage to the Holy Land in her old age, and later tradition credits her with finding the True Cross. She is honored as a saint in the Catholic and Orthodox churches. Whether she was Constantius's wife or concubine is debated.
