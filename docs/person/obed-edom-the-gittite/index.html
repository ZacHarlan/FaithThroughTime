Obed-edom the Gittite

c. 1080 BC

Man of Gath in whose house the ark rested three months after Uzzah's death. God blessed his household, which encouraged David to bring the ark on to Jerusalem. Chronicles later lists an Obed-edom among the Levite gatekeepers and singers, and many readers take them to be the same man; others distinguish him from Obed-edom son of Jeduthun.
