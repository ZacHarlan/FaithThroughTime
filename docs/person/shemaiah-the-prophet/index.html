Person · Prophet

Shemaiah the prophet

dates unknown

Prophet in the reign of Rehoboam. He told Rehoboam not to fight the northern tribes after the kingdom split, and later told the princes of Judah that Shishak's invasion was judgement, whereupon they humbled themselves. Chronicles says he wrote an account of Rehoboam's acts.
