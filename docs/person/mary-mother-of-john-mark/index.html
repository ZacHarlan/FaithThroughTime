Person · Disciple

Mary, mother of John Mark

c. 5 BC

Jerusalem believer in whose house many were praying when Peter came knocking after his release from prison (Acts 12:12). Her son was John, surnamed Mark. Colossians 4:10 calls Mark "sister's son to Barnabas" (KJV; the Greek anepsios is often rendered "cousin"), which makes her Barnabas's sister (or, if the word means cousin, his relative).
