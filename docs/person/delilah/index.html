Person · Other

Delilah

c. 1145–1100 BC

A woman of the valley of Sorek whom Samson loved. Bribed by the Philistine lords, she drew out the secret of his strength and betrayed him (Judg 16:4-21); Scripture does not say whether she was a Philistine or an Israelite.
