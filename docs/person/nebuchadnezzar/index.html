Person · Ruler

Nebuchadnezzar

c. 634–562 BC

The Babylonian king who besieged Jerusalem, destroyed Solomon's temple, and carried Judah into exile, yet appears in Daniel as a ruler humbled to confess the Most High. The Babylonian Chronicle preserves his own court's terse record of the first capture of the city.
