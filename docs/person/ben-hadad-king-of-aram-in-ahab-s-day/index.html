Person · Ruler

Ben-hadad, king of Aram in Ahab's day

d. c. 885 BC

King of Aram who besieged Samaria with thirty-two kings and was beaten twice by Ahab, who then let him go under a covenant (1 Kgs 20). Later, sick, he sent Hazael to ask Elisha whether he would recover; Hazael smothered him and reigned in his place (2 Kgs 8:7-15).
