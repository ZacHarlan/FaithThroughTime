Person · Ruler

Empress Irene

c. AD 752–803

Byzantine empress who ruled as regent for her son from 780 and in her own right from 797 to 802. She summoned the Second Council of Nicaea in 787, which restored the veneration of icons. The Orthodox Church honours her as a saint.
