Person · Ancestor

Heli

c. 60 BC

Named in Luke 3:23 as the father of Joseph, in the line running back through Matthat and Levi to Nathan, a son of David. Matthew 1:16 gives Joseph's father as Jacob instead, and the table keeps Matthew's link as Joseph's father_id and shows Heli's link as a separate parent relationship. Two classic explanations are offered. Africanus held that Heli and Jacob were half-brothers, Heli dying childless so that Jacob fathered Joseph by levirate marriage and Joseph counted as Heli's heir. Others hold that Luke gives Mary's ancestry, making Heli Mary's father and Joseph his son-in-law. Scripture does not say which is right.
