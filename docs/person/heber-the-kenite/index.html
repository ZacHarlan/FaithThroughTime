Person · Other

Heber the Kenite

c. 1320 BC

Kenite, of the family of Hobab, Moses' father-in-law. He had separated from the other Kenites and pitched his tent near Kedesh, at peace with Jabin of Hazor; his wife Jael killed Sisera.
