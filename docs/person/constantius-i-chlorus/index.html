Person · Ruler

Constantius I Chlorus

c. AD 250–306

Roman emperor in the West (Caesar from 293, Augustus 305-306), father of Constantine the Great. His eastern colleagues carried out the Great Persecution, but his own rule in Gaul and Britain was mild. He died at York, where his troops proclaimed Constantine.
