Person · Prophet

Jeremiah

c. 650–570 BC

The weeping prophet who warned Judah for decades of coming Babylonian judgment, endured imprisonment and a muddy cistern, and promised a new covenant written on the heart. Clay bullae from the City of David bear names of royal officials, such as Jehucal son of Shelemiah, who appear in his book.
