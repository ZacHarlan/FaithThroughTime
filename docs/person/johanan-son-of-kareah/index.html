Person · Warrior

Johanan son of Kareah

c. 630 BC

Commander of Judah's forces in the fields who warned Gedaliah of the plot against him, then pursued Ishmael after the murder. Despite Jeremiah's warning from the LORD, he led the remnant to Egypt and took Jeremiah and Baruch with them.
