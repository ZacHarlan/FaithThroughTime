Diet of Worms

AD 1521

Summoned before Emperor Charles V, Martin Luther refused to recant his writings, declaring his conscience captive to the Word of God. Outlawed by the Edict of Worms (May 1521), he was sheltered at Wartburg Castle, where he translated the New Testament into German from the Greek.
