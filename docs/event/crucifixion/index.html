Crucifixion

c. AD 33

Condemned under Pontius Pilate, Jesus is crucified at Golgotha outside Jerusalem at Passover, and the Gospels present his death as an atoning sacrifice foretold by the prophets. Pilate is attested by an inscription found at Caesarea, and a crucified man's heel bone from Jerusalem confirms the Roman practice.
