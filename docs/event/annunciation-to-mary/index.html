Annunciation to Mary

5 BC

The angel Gabriel announces to Mary at Nazareth that she will conceive by the Holy Spirit and bear a son named Jesus, and Mary consents. She then visits her relative Elizabeth, who is six months pregnant with John. Matthew tells of the angel's message to Joseph in a dream.
