Death of John the Baptist

c. AD 32

Herod Antipas has John imprisoned for condemning his marriage to Herodias, his brother Philip's former wife. At Herod's birthday banquet Herodias's daughter pleases him with a dance and, prompted by her mother, asks for John's head, which is carried in on a platter. John's disciples bury the body and tell Jesus. Josephus names the fortress of Machaerus as the prison.
