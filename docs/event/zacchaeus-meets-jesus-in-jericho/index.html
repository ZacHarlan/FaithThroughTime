Zacchaeus Meets Jesus in Jericho

c. AD 33

As Jesus passes through Jericho on the way to Jerusalem, Zacchaeus, a wealthy chief tax collector too short to see over the crowd, climbs a sycamore tree. Jesus calls him down and stays at his house; Zacchaeus gives half his goods to the poor and repays fourfold. Jesus declares that salvation has come to the house.
