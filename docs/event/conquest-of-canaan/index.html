Conquest of Canaan

1451–1445 BC

Under Joshua, Israel crosses the Jordan and campaigns through Canaan, defeating coalitions of kings and allotting the land among the twelve tribes. Destruction layers at sites like Hazor, the greatest Canaanite city of the region, fuel lively scholarly debate over the conquest's scope and character.
