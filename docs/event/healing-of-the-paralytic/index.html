Event · Miracle

Healing of the Paralytic

AD 31

In Capernaum, four friends lowered a paralyzed man through the roof to reach Jesus, who first forgave his sins and then commanded him to walk. Matthew, Mark, and Luke record the miracle, which joined Jesus' power to heal with his authority to forgive; Capernaum excavations reveal the crowded basalt houses such a scene assumes.
