Tabernacle Completed

1490 BC

Following the pattern revealed on Sinai, Israel completes the tabernacle, a portable tent sanctuary where God's glory descends to dwell at the center of the camp. Its gilded frames recall Egyptian portable shrines of the era, while its design anticipates the temple and, for Christians, the incarnation.
