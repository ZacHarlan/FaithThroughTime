Tiglath-pileser Takes Damascus and Galilee

c. 740 BC

Answering Ahaz's call, Tiglath-pileser of Assyria captures Damascus and kills Rezin, and carries off the people of Galilee and Gilead. Ahaz goes to meet him at Damascus and copies its altar for the temple.
