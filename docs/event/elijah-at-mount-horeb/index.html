Elijah at Mount Horeb

c. 904 BC

Fleeing Jezebel's threat, Elijah walks to Horeb, where the Lord comes to him not in wind, earthquake or fire but in a still small voice. He is sent to anoint Hazael, Jehu and Elisha, and finds Elisha ploughing.
