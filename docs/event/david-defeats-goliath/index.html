David Defeats Goliath

c. 1063 BC

In the valley of Elah the Philistine champion Goliath of Gath defies the armies of Israel for forty days. The young David, sent by Jesse with food for his brothers, faces him in the name of the LORD and kills him with a sling stone, and Israel routs the Philistines.
