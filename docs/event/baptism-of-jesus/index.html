Baptism of Jesus

c. AD 29

Jesus is baptized by John in the Jordan River, where the Spirit descends like a dove and a voice from heaven declares him the beloved Son. The moment inaugurates his public ministry; John himself is independently attested by the historian Josephus, who records his baptizing movement and death.
