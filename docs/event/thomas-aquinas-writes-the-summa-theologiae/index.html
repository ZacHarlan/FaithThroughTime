Thomas Aquinas Writes the Summa Theologiae

c. AD 1265–1273

Aquinas works on his Summa Theologiae from about 1265 until he stops writing in December 1273, leaving the third part unfinished at his death in 1274. It became one of the most influential works of Christian theology, setting out faith and reason in a single systematic plan.
