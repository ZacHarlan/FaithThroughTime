Resurrection

AD 33

On the third day, women find Jesus' tomb empty and meet the risen Lord, who then appears to his disciples over forty days. Paul preserves an early creed listing eyewitnesses of the resurrection, the event Christians confess as death's defeat and the firstfruits of the new creation.
