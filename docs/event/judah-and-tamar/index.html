Judah and Tamar

c. 1708 BC

Judah fails to give his widowed daughter-in-law Tamar to his youngest son, and Tamar secures her rights by a bold ruse; Judah himself concedes, "She hath been more righteous than I." Her twin sons Perez and Zerah are born, and Perez stands in the line of David and of Jesus (Matt 1:3).
