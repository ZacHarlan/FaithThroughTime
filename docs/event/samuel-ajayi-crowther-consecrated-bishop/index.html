Samuel Ajayi Crowther Consecrated Bishop

AD 1864

Crowther, born in Yorubaland, enslaved as a boy, rescued by the Royal Navy and educated in Sierra Leone, is consecrated at Canterbury Cathedral on 29 June 1864. He is the first African Anglican bishop and oversees the Niger mission. He also translated the Bible into Yoruba.
