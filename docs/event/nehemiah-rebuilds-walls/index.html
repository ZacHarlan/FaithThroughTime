Nehemiah Rebuilds Walls

445 BC

Nehemiah, cupbearer to the Persian king, returns to Jerusalem and rallies the people to rebuild the ruined walls in fifty-two days despite armed opposition from Sanballat and Tobiah. The Elephantine papyri independently name Sanballat as governor of Samaria, anchoring the memoir in Persian-era history.
