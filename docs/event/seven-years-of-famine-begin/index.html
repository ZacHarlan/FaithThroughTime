Seven Years of Famine Begin

1708 BC

After seven years of plenty, famine spreads across Egypt and the nearby lands, and Joseph opens the storehouses. Jacob's sons come twice to buy grain; on the second journey, when two years of the famine have passed and five remain, Joseph reveals himself to them (Gen 45:6).
