Hezekiah's Deliverance

713 BC

When Sennacherib's armies overrun Judah, Hezekiah spreads the Assyrian threat before the Lord and Isaiah promises rescue; the invader withdraws with Jerusalem untaken. Sennacherib's prism boasts of shutting Hezekiah up like a caged bird yet claims no capture, while the Lachish reliefs depict the campaign's fury.
