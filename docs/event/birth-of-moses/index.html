Birth of Moses

1571 BC

In the days when Pharaoh ordered every newborn Hebrew boy thrown into the Nile, a Levite woman hides her son for three months and then sets him afloat in a basket of reeds. Pharaoh's daughter finds him and raises him as her own, with his own mother as nurse.
