Gamaliel's Counsel before the Sanhedrin

AD 33

After the apostles are arrested and freed by an angel, they are brought before the council and Peter answers that they must obey God rather than men. When the council wants to kill them, the Pharisee Gamaliel advises restraint: if the movement is of men it will come to nothing, but if of God no one can overthrow it. They beat the apostles and release them.
