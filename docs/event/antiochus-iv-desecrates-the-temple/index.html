Antiochus IV Desecrates the Temple

167 BC

Antiochus IV Epiphanes forbids circumcision, the Sabbath and the Law, and in December 167 BC an altar to Zeus is set up on the altar of burnt offering in the Jerusalem temple. Daniel 11:31 is often read as pointing to these acts. The outrage leads directly to the Maccabean Revolt.
