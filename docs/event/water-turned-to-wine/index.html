Event · Miracle

Water Turned to Wine

c. AD 30

At a wedding in Cana of Galilee, prompted by his mother Mary, Jesus turned water in six stone purification jars into choice wine. John alone records this first sign, which revealed Jesus' glory to his disciples and inaugurated his public ministry with an image of messianic abundance.
