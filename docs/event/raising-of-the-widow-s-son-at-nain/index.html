Raising of the Widow's Son at Nain

AD 31

Meeting a funeral procession at the gate of Nain, Jesus was moved by a widow who had lost her only son, touched the bier, and restored the young man to life. Found only in Luke, the miracle echoes Elijah's raising of the widow's son at Zarephath and led the crowd to hail a great prophet.
