Crossing the Red Sea

1491 BC

Trapped between Pharaoh's chariots and the sea, Israel watches God part the waters at Moses' outstretched hand, crossing on dry ground while the pursuing army is drowned. The Song of the Sea celebrating this rescue is reckoned among the oldest poetry preserved in the Hebrew Bible.
