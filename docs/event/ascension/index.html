Event · Miracle

Ascension

c. AD 33

Forty days after the resurrection, Jesus blesses his disciples on the Mount of Olives and is taken up into heaven, commissioning them as witnesses to the ends of the earth. Angels declare he will return as he departed, framing the church's mission between promise and hope.
