Martyrdom of Polycarp

c. AD 155

Bishop Polycarp of Smyrna is put to death in the stadium at Smyrna, saying "Eighty-six years I have served Him, and He never did me any injury." In the earliest account the flames did not consume him and he was finally stabbed.
