Hudson Taylor Founds the China Inland Mission

AD 1865

Hudson Taylor founds the China Inland Mission in 1865 to carry the gospel to Chinas inland provinces. It is non-denominational and depends on prayer rather than appeals for funds, and by 1900 it is the largest mission in China.
