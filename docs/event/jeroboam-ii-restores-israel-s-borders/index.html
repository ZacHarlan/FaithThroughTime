Jeroboam II Restores Israel's Borders

c. 800–784 BC

Jeroboam II, though he continues the sin of Jeroboam, restores Israel's coast from Hamath to the Dead Sea as Jonah had foretold. It is the northern kingdom's last season of power and wealth, which Amos and Hosea would condemn.
