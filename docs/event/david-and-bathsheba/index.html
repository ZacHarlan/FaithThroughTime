David and Bathsheba

1035 BC

David sees Bathsheba bathing, takes her, and arranges the death of her husband Uriah at the siege of Rabbah. The prophet Nathan confronts him with the story of the ewe lamb, and David confesses (Ps 51); the child dies, and Solomon is born to them later.
