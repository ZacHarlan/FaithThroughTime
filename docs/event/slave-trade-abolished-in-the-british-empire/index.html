Slave Trade Abolished in the British Empire

AD 1807

Parliament passes the Slave Trade Act on 25 March 1807 after twenty years of campaigning led by William Wilberforce and many others, especially evangelical Christians and Quakers. Slavery itself was abolished across the empire in 1833, days before Wilberforce died.
