Transfiguration

c. AD 32

On a high mountain, Peter, James, and John saw Jesus transfigured in dazzling glory, conversing with Moses and Elijah as a voice from the cloud declared him the beloved Son. All three synoptics record the scene, which links law and prophets to Jesus; 2 Peter appeals to it as eyewitness testimony.
