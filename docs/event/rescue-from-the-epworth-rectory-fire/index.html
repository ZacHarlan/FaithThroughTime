Rescue from the Epworth Rectory Fire

AD 1709

On 9 February 1709 the rectory at Epworth burns down, and five-year-old John Wesley is pulled from an upstairs window. Susanna Wesley, who taught her children at home, saw it as Gods special care for him, and Wesley later called himself a brand plucked from the burning.
