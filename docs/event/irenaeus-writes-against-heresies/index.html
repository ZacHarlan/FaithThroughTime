Irenaeus Writes Against Heresies

c. AD 180

Irenaeus of Lyon writes Against Heresies (the Greek original is largely lost; a complete Latin translation survives), refuting Gnostic teaching and appealing to Scripture, the rule of faith and the succession of bishops from the apostles.
