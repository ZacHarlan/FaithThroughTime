Jonathan Edwards and the First Great Awakening

c. AD 1734–1743

Revival in Jonathan Edwards' Northampton church (1734-35) and George Whitefield's preaching tours from 1739 spread the First Great Awakening through the American colonies, alongside the Evangelical Revival in Britain. Edwards' sermon "Sinners in the Hands of an Angry God" (1741) became its best-known sermon.
