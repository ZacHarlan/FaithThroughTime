Fall of Babylon to Cyrus

539 BC

Babylon falls to the Medes and Persians in a single night. Belshazzar is killed after the writing on the wall is read at his feast, and Darius the Mede takes the kingdom (Dan 5). The Nabonidus Chronicle independently records the city's capture without a battle.
