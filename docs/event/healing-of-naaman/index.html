Healing of Naaman

c. 890 BC

Naaman, commander of Aram's army, sought healing from leprosy on the word of a captive Israelite servant girl. Directed by the prophet Elisha to wash seven times in the Jordan, he was cleansed and confessed Israel's God; Jesus later cited him at Nazareth as a Gentile who received God's mercy.
