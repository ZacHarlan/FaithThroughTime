Ezra Reads the Law at the Water Gate

c. 445 BC

On the first day of the seventh month the people gather in the square before the Water Gate and ask Ezra the scribe to bring the book of the law of Moses. From morning until midday he reads it, and the Levites give the sense so that all understand. The people weep, then keep the Feast of Booths with joy, and a solemn assembly follows.
