Athanasius Lists the 27 NT Books

AD 367

In his thirty-ninth Festal Letter from Alexandria, Bishop Athanasius listed exactly the twenty-seven books of today's New Testament, the earliest surviving list to match it precisely. Calling them the springs of salvation, he distinguished canonical Scripture from disputed and spurious writings.
