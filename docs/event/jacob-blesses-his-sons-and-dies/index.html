Jacob Blesses His Sons and Dies

1689 BC

Gathered at his bedside in Egypt, Jacob adopts Joseph's sons Ephraim and Manasseh and pronounces a blessing over each of his twelve sons, including the promise that the sceptre will not depart from Judah. He charges them to bury him at Machpelah and dies; Joseph and his brothers carry him to Canaan.
