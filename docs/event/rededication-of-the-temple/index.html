Rededication of the Temple

164 BC

On 25 Kislev, three years after its desecration, Judas Maccabeus cleanses the temple, builds a new altar and restores the daily offerings with eight days of celebration (1 Macc 4:36-59; 2 Macc 10:1-8). The feast of Dedication (Hanukkah) that remembers it is named in John 10:22.
