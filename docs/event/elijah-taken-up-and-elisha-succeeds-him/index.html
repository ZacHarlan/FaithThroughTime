Elijah Taken Up and Elisha Succeeds Him

896 BC

At the Jordan Elijah parts the water and is carried up to heaven in a whirlwind with a chariot and horses of fire. Elisha takes up his fallen mantle, asks for a double portion of his spirit and parts the river again.
