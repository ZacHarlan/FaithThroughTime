Event · Miracle

Pentecost

c. AD 33

On the feast of Pentecost, fifty days after Passover, the Holy Spirit descends on the disciples with rushing wind and tongues of fire, and Peter's sermon draws about three thousand into the newborn church. Falling on Shavuot, the harvest festival, the event launches the gospel's spread from Jerusalem.
