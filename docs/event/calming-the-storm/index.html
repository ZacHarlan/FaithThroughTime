Event · Miracle

Calming the Storm

c. AD 31

When a sudden squall threatened to swamp the disciples' boat on the Sea of Galilee, Jesus rose from sleep and stilled wind and waves with a word. All three synoptic gospels record the awed question of who this could be; a first-century fishing boat excavated from the lake's mud vividly illustrates the scene.
