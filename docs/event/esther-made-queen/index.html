Esther Made Queen

c. 479 BC

Esther, a Jewish orphan raised by her cousin Mordecai, is taken to Ahasuerus at Susa in the tenth month of his seventh year. He loves her above the other candidates, sets the royal crown on her head and makes her queen in place of Vashti. Esther keeps her people and family secret at Mordecai's direction.
