Giving of the Law

1491 BC

Amid thunder and fire on Mount Sinai, God speaks the Ten Commandments and gives Moses the Torah, constituting rescued slaves as a kingdom of priests and a holy nation. Scholars note that the covenant's structure parallels ancient suzerainty treaties, casting God as Israel's sovereign King.
