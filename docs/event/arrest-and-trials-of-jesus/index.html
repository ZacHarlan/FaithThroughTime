Arrest and Trials of Jesus

AD 33

After praying in Gethsemane, Jesus is arrested by a party guided by Judas and taken to Annas and Caiaphas and before the council, while Peter denies him three times. In the morning he is brought to Pilate, who sends him to Herod Antipas and then, after offering to release him instead of Barabbas, hands him over to be crucified.
