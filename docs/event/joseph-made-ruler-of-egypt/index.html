Joseph Made Ruler of Egypt

1715 BC

Sold by his brothers yet raised from prison to Pharaoh's right hand, Joseph interprets dreams of coming famine and administers Egypt's grain stores. His rise turns betrayal into rescue, positioning him to preserve Jacob's family, the fledgling covenant line, through seven years of scarcity.
