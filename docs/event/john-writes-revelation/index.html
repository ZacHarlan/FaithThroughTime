Event · Scripture

John Writes Revelation

c. AD 95

Exiled on the rocky island of Patmos, John received sweeping visions of the risen Christ, letters for seven churches of Asia Minor, and the final triumph of the Lamb. Written to believers under Roman pressure, it closes the canon with the promise of a new heaven and new earth; Irenaeus placed it late in Domitian's reign.
