Raising of Jairus' Daughter

c. AD 31

Jairus, a synagogue leader, begged Jesus to save his dying daughter, and though word came that she had died, Jesus took her hand and raised her. Mark preserves the original Aramaic command, Talitha koum, and all three synoptics interweave the story with the healing of the hemorrhaging woman.
