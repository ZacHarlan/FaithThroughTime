Elijah on Mount Carmel

c. 905 BC

On Mount Carmel, Elijah stands alone against the prophets of Baal, and fire from heaven consumes his water-drenched sacrifice, turning Israel's heart back to the Lord. The contest confronts Baal, the Canaanite storm god, on his own claimed terrain of fire and rain, and ends a long drought at God's word.
