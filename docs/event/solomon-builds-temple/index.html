Solomon Builds Temple

1012–1005 BC

Solomon builds the first temple on Jerusalem's Mount Moriah, a cedar-lined house of hewn stone where the ark comes to rest and God's glory fills the sanctuary at its dedication. Its long-room, tripartite plan resembles excavated Syrian temples such as Ain Dara, rooting it firmly in its ancient world.
