The Holy Club at Oxford

c. AD 1729–1735

Charles Wesley and friends meet at Oxford for study, prayer, Communion and visiting prisoners. John Wesley soon leads the group, and its disciplined method earns the nickname Methodists. George Whitefield joins a few years later.
