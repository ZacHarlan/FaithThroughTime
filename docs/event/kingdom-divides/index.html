Kingdom Divides

975 BC

After Solomon's death, Rehoboam's harsh answer at Shechem drives ten northern tribes to crown Jeroboam, splitting the nation into Israel and Judah. The rupture sets the two kingdoms on divergent paths, and Pharaoh Shishak's invasion, carved at Karnak, soon exploited their weakness.
