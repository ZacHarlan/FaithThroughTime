Death of Samson

c. 1117 BC

Blinded and set to grind in the prison at Gaza after Delilah's betrayal, Samson is brought out to entertain the Philistines at a feast to Dagon. Praying for strength one last time, he pulls down the pillars of the temple, killing more Philistines at his death than in his life.
