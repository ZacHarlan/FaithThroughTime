Event · Kingdom

Vashti Deposed

c. 483 BC

At the end of a six-month display of the wealth of his empire and a feast at Susa, king Ahasuerus (Xerxes) summons Queen Vashti, and she refuses to come. On the advice of his counsellors he deposes her and issues a decree to every province, which clears the way for a new queen.
