Event · Death

Absalom Kills Amnon

c. 1031 BC

Amnon wrongs his half-sister Tamar, and her brother Absalom hides his anger for two years before having Amnon killed at a sheepshearing feast. Absalom flees to Geshur, and David mourns for both sons.
