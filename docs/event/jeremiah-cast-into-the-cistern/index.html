Jeremiah Cast into the Cistern

589 BC

Accused of weakening the soldiers' morale, Jeremiah is thrown into a muddy cistern. Ebed-melech, an Ethiopian court official, has him lifted out, and Zedekiah secretly asks him again for God's word.
