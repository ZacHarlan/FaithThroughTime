Death of Saul at Gilboa

1055 BC

Routed by the Philistines on Mount Gilboa, with Jonathan and two other sons already dead, the wounded Saul falls on his own sword. The men of Jabesh-gilead recover the bodies from the wall of Beth-shan and bury them.
