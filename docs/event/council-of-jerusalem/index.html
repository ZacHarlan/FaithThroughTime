Council of Jerusalem

AD 52

Apostles and elders gathered to decide whether Gentile converts must be circumcised and keep the law of Moses. After Peter testified that Jews and Gentiles alike are saved through the grace of the Lord Jesus (Acts 15:11) and James gave his judgment, the council freed Gentile believers from circumcision and asked them to abstain from food offered to idols, blood, things strangled and sexual immorality (Acts 15:20, 29).
