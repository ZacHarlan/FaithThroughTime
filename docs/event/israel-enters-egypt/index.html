Israel Enters Egypt

1706 BC

Jacob and his whole household migrate to Egypt at Joseph's invitation, settling in the fertile land of Goshen and growing there into a numerous people. Egyptian art such as the Beni Hasan tomb painting depicts Semitic families entering Egypt, illustrating this well-traveled path in times of famine.
