James Killed and Peter Imprisoned by Agrippa I

AD 44

Herod Agrippa I has James the brother of John killed with the sword and, seeing that it pleased the people, arrests Peter during the days of unleavened bread. An angel frees Peter, who goes to the house of Mary the mother of John Mark. Soon afterwards Agrippa is struck down at Caesarea and dies.
