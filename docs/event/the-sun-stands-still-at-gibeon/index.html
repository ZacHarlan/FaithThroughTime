The Sun Stands Still at Gibeon

c. 1450 BC

Five Amorite kings attack Gibeon, which had made peace with Israel. Joshua marches up overnight and, as the enemy flees, asks the Lord that the sun and moon stand still; the day is lengthened and the kings are defeated.
