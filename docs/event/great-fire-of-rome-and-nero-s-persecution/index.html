Great Fire of Rome and Nero's Persecution

AD 64

In July AD 64 a fire destroys much of Rome. Rumour blames Nero, who fastens the guilt on the Christians and has many of them put to death with great cruelty (Tacitus). It is the first known Roman state violence against Christians.
