Helena Journeys to the Holy Land

c. AD 327–328

Helena, mother of Constantine, travels to Jerusalem and Palestine and supports church building at sites tied to the life of Christ. Later tradition says she found the True Cross; this is a legend first recorded decades afterward, not something Eusebius reports. Constantines churches at the Holy Sepulchre and Bethlehem belong to the same programme.
