Herod Begins Rebuilding the Temple

c. 19 BC

Herod the Great begins to rebuild and enlarge the Jerusalem temple in the eighteenth year of his reign, with the sanctuary itself finished in about eighteen months and the surrounding courts and porches continuing for decades. The Jews tell Jesus the work has been going on forty-six years.
