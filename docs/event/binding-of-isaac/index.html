Event · Covenant

Binding of Isaac

c. 1872 BC

God tests Abraham by commanding the sacrifice of Isaac, the son of promise, on Mount Moriah; at the final moment an angel stays the knife and a ram is offered instead. Known in Jewish tradition as the Akedah, the event affirms that God himself provides the sacrifice, a theme Christians hear echoed at Calvary.
