Famine Relief Sent from Antioch to Judea

AD 44

The prophet Agabus comes to Antioch and foretells a great famine, which comes in the reign of Claudius. The disciples there decide each according to his means to send relief to the believers in Judea, and Barnabas and Saul carry the gift to the elders at Jerusalem.
