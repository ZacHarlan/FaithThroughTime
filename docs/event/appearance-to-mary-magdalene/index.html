Event · Miracle

Appearance to Mary Magdalene

c. AD 33

Weeping at the empty tomb, Mary Magdalene mistook the risen Jesus for the gardener until he spoke her name, and she answered, Rabbouni. John records this first appearance, and Jesus' commission made her the first herald of the resurrection, honored in tradition as apostle to the apostles.
