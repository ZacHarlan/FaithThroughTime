Death of John

c. AD 100

According to early tradition (Irenaeus, Eusebius), John lived at Ephesus into the reign of Trajan (from AD 98) and died there, the last of the Twelve; his death is taken to close the Apostolic Age. Scripture records nothing about it (John 21:23 only corrects a rumour that he would not die).
