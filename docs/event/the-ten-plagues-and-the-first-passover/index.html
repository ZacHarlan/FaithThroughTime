The Ten Plagues and the First Passover

1491 BC

When Pharaoh refuses to let Israel go, ten plagues strike Egypt, from the Nile turned to blood to the death of the firstborn. Israel is spared by the blood of the Passover lamb on their doorposts, and Pharaoh at last sends them out; the feast is kept as a lasting memorial.
