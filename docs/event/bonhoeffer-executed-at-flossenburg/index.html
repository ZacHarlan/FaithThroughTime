Bonhoeffer Executed at Flossenburg

AD 1945

Dietrich Bonhoeffer, a pastor in the Confessing Church who joined the plot against Hitler, is hanged on 9 April 1945, a month before the war ended. His Cost of Discipleship and Letters and Papers from Prison remain widely read.
