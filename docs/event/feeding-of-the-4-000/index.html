Feeding of the 4,000

AD 32

In the largely Gentile Decapolis region, Jesus fed a crowd that had stayed with him three days, multiplying seven loaves and a few fish with seven baskets remaining. Recorded by Matthew and Mark as distinct from the earlier feeding, it signals that the messianic banquet extends beyond Israel to the nations.
