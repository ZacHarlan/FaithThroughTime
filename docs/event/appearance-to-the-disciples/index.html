Appearance to the Disciples

AD 33

On the evening of the resurrection, Jesus stood among his disciples behind locked doors, greeted them with peace, and showed them his hands and side. Luke notes that he ate broiled fish to prove he was no ghost, and John records him breathing on them the Holy Spirit and sending them as the Father sent him.
