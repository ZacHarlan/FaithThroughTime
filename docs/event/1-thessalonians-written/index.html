Event · Scripture

1 Thessalonians Written

c. AD 51

Paul writes from Corinth to encourage the young Thessalonian church, with teaching on the return of Christ. It is among the earliest of Paul's letters: the earliest on most reckonings, though Galatians comes first on the South Galatian view followed in this timeline.
