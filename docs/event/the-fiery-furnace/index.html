The Fiery Furnace

c. 580 BC

Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego refuse to bow to Nebuchadnezzar's golden image and are thrown into a furnace heated seven times hotter. The king sees four men walking in the fire, one like a son of the gods, and brings them out unharmed.
