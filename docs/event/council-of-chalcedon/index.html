Council of Chalcedon

AD 451

Bishops meeting at Chalcedon defined that Christ is one person in two natures, fully God and fully man, without confusion or separation. Guided by Pope Leo's Tome against Eutyches' teaching, the definition became the standard of Christology for the Catholic, Eastern Orthodox and most Protestant churches; the Oriental Orthodox churches did not accept it, and the division has lasted.
