Syro-Ephraimite War

c. 742–740 BC

Rezin of Aram and Pekah of Israel march on Jerusalem to force Ahaz into an alliance against Assyria. Isaiah meets Ahaz with the sign of Immanuel and tells him to trust God, but Ahaz sends tribute to Tiglath-pileser instead.
