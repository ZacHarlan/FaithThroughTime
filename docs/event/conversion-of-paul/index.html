Event · Miracle

Conversion of Paul

c. AD 35

On the Damascus road, Saul of Tarsus, traveling to arrest followers of Jesus, was confronted by a blinding vision of the risen Christ. Acts recounts the event three times and Paul's letters confirm it, marking the turning point that made the church's fiercest persecutor its apostle to the Gentiles.
