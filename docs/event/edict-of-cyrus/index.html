Edict of Cyrus

536 BC

Cyrus of Persia, having taken Babylon, decrees that the exiled Judeans may return to Jerusalem to rebuild the house of the Lord, restoring the plundered temple vessels. The Cyrus Cylinder reflects his wider policy of restoring displaced peoples and shrines, and Isaiah strikingly names him God's anointed.
