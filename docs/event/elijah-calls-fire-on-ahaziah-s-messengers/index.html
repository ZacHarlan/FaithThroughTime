Elijah Calls Fire on Ahaziah's Messengers

c. 897 BC

Ahaziah of Israel, injured in a fall, sends to ask Baal-zebub of Ekron whether he will recover. Elijah intercepts the messengers with the Lord's rebuke, and fire falls on two companies of soldiers sent to seize him before the third captain begs for mercy.
