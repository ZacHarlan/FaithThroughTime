Healing of the Gerasene Demoniac

c. AD 31

In the Decapolis east of the Sea of Galilee, Jesus freed a tomb-dwelling man possessed by a legion of demons, which entered a herd of pigs and perished in the sea. Sent home rather than allowed to follow, the man became the first herald of Jesus in Gentile lands; manuscripts vary between Gerasenes, Gadarenes, and Gergesenes.
