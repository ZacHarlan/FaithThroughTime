Jonah's Mission to Nineveh

c. 771 BC

Called to warn Nineveh, Jonah flees by sea, is swallowed by a great fish and prays from its belly. Sent again, he preaches in the Assyrian capital, the city repents, and God relents, to Jonah's anger and the Lord's rebuke.
