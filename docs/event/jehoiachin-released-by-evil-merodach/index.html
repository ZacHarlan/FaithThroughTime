Jehoiachin Released by Evil-merodach

c. 562 BC

In the 37th year of his exile, Evil-merodach lifts Jehoiachin out of prison, gives him a seat above other kings and a place at his table for life. The book of Kings ends on this sign that David's line is not finished.
