David Anointed by Samuel

c. 1063 BC

God sends Samuel to Bethlehem to anoint David, Jesse's youngest son and a shepherd, declaring that the Lord looks on the heart rather than outward stature. The Tel Dan inscription's reference to the house of David gives his dynasty its earliest extrabiblical attestation.
