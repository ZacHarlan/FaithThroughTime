Augustines Conversion and Baptism

c. AD 386–387

In a garden at Milan in 386, Augustine hears a childs voice say take up and read, opens Romans 13, and is converted. With his son Adeodatus he is baptised by Ambrose at Easter 387. He describes it in his Confessions; his mother Monica died soon after, on the way back to Africa.
