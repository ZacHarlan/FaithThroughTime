Event · Judgment

Fall of Jerusalem

588 BC

Nebuchadnezzar's Babylonian army breaches Jerusalem after a long siege, burning the temple, razing the walls, and deporting Judah's people. The Babylonian Chronicle independently records his earlier seizure of the city, corroborating the biblical account of Judah's collapse and exile.
