Balaam and Balak

1452 BC

Balak, king of Moab, hires the seer Balaam to curse Israel camped on the plains of Moab. Balaam's donkey sees the angel he cannot, and each time he opens his mouth he can only bless, including a prophecy of a star out of Jacob and a sceptre out of Israel.
