Martin Luther Posts the 95 Theses

AD 1517

Martin Luther challenged the sale of indulgences with ninety-five theses for academic debate, which he sent to Archbishop Albert of Mainz; that he also posted them on the Wittenberg castle church door is traditional. Printers spread them across Germany within weeks, and they became the opening of the Reformation.
