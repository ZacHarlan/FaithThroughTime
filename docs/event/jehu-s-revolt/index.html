Jehu's Revolt

884 BC

Elisha sends a young prophet to anoint Jehu, an army commander at Ramoth-gilead. Jehu drives to Jezreel and kills Jehoram of Israel and Ahaziah of Judah, Jezebel is thrown from her window, and Ahab's seventy sons and the priests of Baal are destroyed.
