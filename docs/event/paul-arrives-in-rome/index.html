Paul Arrives in Rome

AD 63–65

After appealing to Caesar, surviving shipwreck at Malta, Paul reached Rome under guard and spent two years in rented lodging, welcoming all and preaching the kingdom unhindered. Acts closes with the gospel proclaimed in the empire's capital, and tradition places several prison epistles in this period.
