Ruth and Boaz

c. 1172–1170 BC

Naomi returns to Bethlehem from Moab with her widowed daughter-in-law Ruth, who gleans in the field of Boaz and wins his notice. Boaz redeems the family's land and marries Ruth, and their son Obed becomes the grandfather of David.
