David Spares Saul at En-gedi

c. 1059 BC

Pursued by Saul with three thousand men, David is hidden in a cave where Saul comes in alone. He cuts off a corner of Saul's robe but refuses to harm the Lord's anointed, and Saul weeps and admits that David will be king.
