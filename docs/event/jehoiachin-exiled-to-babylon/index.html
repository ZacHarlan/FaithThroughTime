Jehoiachin Exiled to Babylon

599 BC

After Jehoiakim's death, Nebuchadnezzar takes Jerusalem, carries King Jehoiachin, his mother and ten thousand leading men to Babylon and makes Zedekiah king. Ezekiel is among the captives.
