Fall of Samaria

721 BC

After a three-year siege, Assyria captures Samaria and extinguishes the northern kingdom, deporting Israelites and resettling foreigners in their land. Sargon II's annals boast of carrying off 27,290 captives, and the scattering of the ten tribes stands as sober vindication of the prophets' warnings.
