Event · Miracle

Walking on Water

c. AD 32

In the night after feeding the multitude, Jesus came to his storm-battered disciples walking across the Sea of Galilee. Matthew, Mark, and John record the event, with Matthew adding Peter's brief walk and sinking; Jesus' reassurance, echoing the divine I am, moved the disciples to worship him as God's Son.
