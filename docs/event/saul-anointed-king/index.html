Saul Anointed King

1095 BC

When the people demand a king like the nations, God directs Samuel to anoint Saul of Benjamin as Israel's first monarch. Tall, striking, and initially victorious over Ammon and the Philistines, Saul forfeits his dynasty through disobedience, opening the way for David's rise.
