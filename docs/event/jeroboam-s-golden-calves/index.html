Jeroboam's Golden Calves

975 BC

Fearing that pilgrimage to Jerusalem would draw his people back to Rehoboam, Jeroboam sets up golden calves at Bethel and Dan and appoints his own priests and feast. The Bible repeatedly names this the sin of Jeroboam that Israel's later kings never abandoned.
