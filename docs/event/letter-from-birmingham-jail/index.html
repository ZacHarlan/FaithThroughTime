Letter from Birmingham Jail

AD 1963

Imprisoned for protesting segregation, Martin Luther King Jr. writes an open letter on 16 April 1963 to white clergy who had urged him to wait. He appeals to Scripture, Augustine and Aquinas to explain why unjust laws must be resisted nonviolently.
