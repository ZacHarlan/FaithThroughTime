Event · Death

Sennacherib Assassinated

681 BC

Sennacherib returns to Nineveh and is killed by his own sons as he worships in the house of Nisroch, and his son Esarhaddon succeeds him. The Bible records it as the end of the king who defied the Lord at Jerusalem.
