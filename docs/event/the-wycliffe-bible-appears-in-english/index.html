The Wycliffe Bible Appears in English

AD 1382

The first complete Bible in English, translated from the Latin Vulgate, appears in the Wycliffe circle: an Early Version c. 1382, largely by Nicholas Hereford, and a smoother Later Version c. 1388-1395 by John Purvey and others. Spread in hand-copied manuscripts by his Lollard followers, it survives in nearly 250 copies; Wycliffe himself was condemned after death.
