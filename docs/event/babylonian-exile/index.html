Babylonian Exile

588–537 BC

Uprooted to Babylon, Judah's exiles mourn Zion by foreign rivers while prophets like Ezekiel and Daniel proclaim God's presence far from the ruined temple. Cuneiform records, including ration tablets naming King Jehoiachin and the Al-Yahudu archive, document the exiled community's life in Babylonia.
