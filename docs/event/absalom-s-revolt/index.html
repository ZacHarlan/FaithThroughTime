Event · War

Absalom's Revolt

1023 BC

Absalom wins the hearts of Israel, has himself proclaimed king at Hebron, and drives David out of Jerusalem. Hushai's counsel defeats Ahithophel's, David's army wins in the forest of Ephraim, and Joab kills Absalom against the king's orders.
