Shishak Invades Judah

c. 971 BC

In Rehoboam's fifth year Shishak of Egypt marches against Jerusalem with a great army. Rehoboam and the princes humble themselves, and the city is spared, but the temple and palace treasures, including Solomon's gold shields, are carried off.
