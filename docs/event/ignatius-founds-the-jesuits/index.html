Ignatius Founds the Jesuits

AD 1540

Pope Paul III approves the Society of Jesus on 27 September 1540. Founded by Ignatius of Loyola and his companions, it centres on education, missions and obedience to the pope and becomes a major force in Catholic renewal and world missions.
