Haggai and Zechariah Rouse the Rebuilding

520 BC

After the work on the temple has stalled for years under local opposition, the word of the LORD comes by Haggai in the second year of Darius, and Zechariah begins to prophesy soon after. Zerubbabel the governor and Jeshua the high priest obey, and the building resumes within a few weeks.
