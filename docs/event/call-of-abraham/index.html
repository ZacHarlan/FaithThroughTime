Call of Abraham

1921 BC

God summons Abram to leave his homeland and his father's house, going by way of Haran, for a country God would show him, promising to make him a great nation and a blessing to all peoples of the earth. His obedient departure launches the covenant story that the rest of Scripture traces through Israel to the Messiah.
