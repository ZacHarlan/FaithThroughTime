Death of Sarah and Purchase of Machpelah

1859 BC

Sarah dies at 127 in Hebron, and Abraham buys the cave of Machpelah from Ephron the Hittite for 400 shekels of silver. It is his only possession in the promised land, and later holds Abraham, Isaac, Rebekah, Leah and Jacob.
