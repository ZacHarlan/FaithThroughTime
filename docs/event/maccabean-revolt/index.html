Maccabean Revolt

167–160 BC

After Antiochus IV desecrates the temple and bans the Law, the priest Mattathias refuses to sacrifice at Modein and raises a revolt (1 Macc 2). His son Judas wins a string of victories, retakes Jerusalem and rededicates the temple in December 164 BC (1 Macc 4:36-59). Judas falls at Elasa in 160 BC.
