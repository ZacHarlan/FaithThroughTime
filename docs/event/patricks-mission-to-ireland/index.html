Patricks Mission to Ireland

c. AD 432

Patrick, a Briton once taken to Ireland as a slave, returns as a missionary bishop and plants churches there. His own Confession is the main source. Tradition puts his arrival in 432 and the founding of Armagh a little later; many modern scholars place his work later in the fifth century.
