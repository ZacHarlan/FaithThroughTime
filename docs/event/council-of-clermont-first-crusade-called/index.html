Council of Clermont — First Crusade Called

AD 1095

Pope Urban II calls on Christians to aid the Byzantine Empire and recover Jerusalem; the exact wording of the speech is known only from later accounts. The First Crusade captured Jerusalem in 1099, beginning two centuries of Crusading warfare.
