Pompey Takes Jerusalem

63 BC

The Roman general Pompey, called in to settle a quarrel between the Hasmonean brothers Hyrcanus II and Aristobulus II, besieges the temple mount for three months and storms it. He enters the Holy of Holies but leaves the treasury untouched. Judea becomes a Roman tributary, the political setting of the Gospels.
