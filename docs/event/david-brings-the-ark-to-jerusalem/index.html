David Brings the Ark to Jerusalem

1045 BC

David brings the ark up from Kirjath-jearim; after Uzzah is struck down it rests three months in the house of Obed-edom, then enters the city of David with sacrifice and dancing. Michal despises David for dancing before the LORD.
