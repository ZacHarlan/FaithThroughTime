Second Temple Built

515 BC

Led by Zerubbabel and Jeshua and stirred by the prophets Haggai and Zechariah, the returned exiles complete a new temple in Jerusalem despite opposition and slender means. Elders wept remembering Solomon's house, yet this second temple would anchor Jewish worship for centuries until the Roman era.
