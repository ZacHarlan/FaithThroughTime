Tower of Babel

c. 2247 BC

United humanity builds a tower on the plain of Shinar to make a name for itself, and God scatters the builders by confusing their language. The story evokes Mesopotamian ziggurats such as Babylon's Etemenanki and explains the dispersion of nations, which Genesis places generations before God's call of Abraham.
