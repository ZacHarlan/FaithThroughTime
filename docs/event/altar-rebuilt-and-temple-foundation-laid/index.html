Altar Rebuilt and Temple Foundation Laid

c. 536–535 BC

In the seventh month the returned exiles gather at Jerusalem, and Jeshua and Zerubbabel set up the altar and restore the daily burnt offerings, though the temple foundation is not yet laid. In the second year of their coming the builders lay the foundation to trumpets and praise; the young shout for joy and the old who remembered the first temple weep.
