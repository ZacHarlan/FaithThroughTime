State of Israel Established

AD 1948

As the British Mandate ended, David Ben-Gurion proclaimed the State of Israel, the first sovereign Jewish state in the land since antiquity. The ingathering of Jewish exiles and the revival of Hebrew as a living national language gave the ancient biblical homeland renewed centrality in Jewish life and Christian reflection.
