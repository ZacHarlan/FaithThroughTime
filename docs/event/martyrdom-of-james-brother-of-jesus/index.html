Martyrdom of James, Brother of Jesus

AD 62

James, leader of the Jerusalem church, is condemned by the high priest Ananus and stoned at Jerusalem while Judea is without a Roman governor. Josephus records the event, and the second-century writer Hegesippus (quoted by Eusebius) gives a fuller account. Scripture does not record it.
