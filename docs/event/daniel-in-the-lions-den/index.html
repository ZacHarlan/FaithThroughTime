Daniel in the Lions' Den

538 BC

Darius the Mede is tricked into a decree forbidding prayer, and Daniel keeps praying three times a day and is thrown to the lions. God shuts their mouths, Daniel's accusers are cast in instead, and Darius honours the God of Daniel.
