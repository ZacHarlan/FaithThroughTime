Tyndale's New Testament Published

AD 1526

William Tyndale published the first printed English New Testament, translated directly from Erasmus' Greek text and smuggled into England in bales of cloth. Though Tyndale was later executed, his vivid phrasing endured, shaping much of the King James New Testament's wording.
