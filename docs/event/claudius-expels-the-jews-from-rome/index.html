Claudius Expels the Jews from Rome

c. AD 49

The emperor Claudius orders all Jews to leave Rome. Among those who go is the couple Aquila and Priscilla, who settle in Corinth and there welcome Paul. The edict is probably tied to disturbances in the Jewish community, which the Roman historian Suetonius links to a certain "Chrestus".
