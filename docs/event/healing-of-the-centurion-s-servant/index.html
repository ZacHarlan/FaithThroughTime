Event · Miracle

Healing of the Centurion's Servant

c. AD 31

A Roman centurion in Capernaum asked Jesus to heal his dying servant, insisting a word from a distance would suffice. Jesus marveled that he had found no such faith in Israel and healed the servant without entering the house; Luke adds that this Gentile officer had built the town's synagogue.
