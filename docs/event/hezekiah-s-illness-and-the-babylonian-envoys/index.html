Hezekiah's Illness and the Babylonian Envoys

c. 713 BC

Told by Isaiah to set his house in order, Hezekiah prays and is given fifteen more years, with the shadow going back ten steps as a sign. Envoys of Merodach-baladan of Babylon then come, and Hezekiah shows them all his treasures, which Isaiah says will one day be carried to Babylon.
