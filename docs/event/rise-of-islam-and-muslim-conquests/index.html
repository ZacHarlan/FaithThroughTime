Rise of Islam and Muslim Conquests

c. AD 632–750

After Muhammad's death (632), Muslim armies conquer Syria, Egypt and North Africa, most of them long Christian lands, and the Sasanian Persian Empire, home to the Church of the East, within about a century, permanently altering the geography of Christianity.
