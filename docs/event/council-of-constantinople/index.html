Council of Constantinople

AD 381

The Second Ecumenical Council, called by Theodosius I, reaffirms Nicaea against Arian and Macedonian views and confesses the deity of the Holy Spirit. The creed now recited as the Nicene Creed (the Niceno-Constantinopolitan Creed) is linked to this council.
