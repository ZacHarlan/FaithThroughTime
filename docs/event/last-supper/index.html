Event · Covenant

Last Supper

AD 33

On the night he was betrayed Jesus eats a final meal with the Twelve, washes their feet (John 13), gives them the bread and the cup as his body and his blood of the new covenant, and foretells Judas's betrayal and Peter's denial. Christians understand the Lord's Supper differently but all trace it to this meal.
