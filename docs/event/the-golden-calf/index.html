The Golden Calf

c. 1491 BC

While Moses is on Sinai receiving the tablets, the people press Aaron to make them gods, and he casts a golden calf. Moses breaks the tablets, destroys the calf, and intercedes for the people; the Levites rally to him, and about three thousand die.
