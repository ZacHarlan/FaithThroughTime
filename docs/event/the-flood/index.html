The Flood

2349–2348 BC

Grieved by human violence, God cleanses the earth with a flood while preserving Noah, his family, and the animals in the ark, then seals a covenant with the rainbow sign. Mesopotamian epics like Gilgamesh and Atrahasis preserve parallel flood tales, yet Genesis uniquely grounds the deluge in divine justice.
