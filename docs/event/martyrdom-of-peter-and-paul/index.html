Martyrdom of Peter and Paul

c. AD 67

Early church tradition holds that Peter and Paul were both put to death at Rome under Nero, Paul by the sword and Peter by crucifixion (1 Clement 5; Eusebius). Scripture does not record either death, though Jesus foretells Peter's (John 21:18-19) and Paul anticipates his own (2 Tim 4:6-8).
