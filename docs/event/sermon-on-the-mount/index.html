Sermon on the Mount

c. AD 31

On a Galilean hillside, Jesus delivers his most famous teaching, opening with the Beatitudes and pressing the Law to its deepest intent on anger, love of enemies, and prayer, including the Lord's Prayer. Matthew presents him as the authoritative teacher of a righteousness surpassing that of the scribes.
