Event · Miracle

Raising of Lazarus

AD 33

At Bethany, Jesus called Lazarus out of the tomb four days after his death, having declared to Martha that he is the resurrection and the life. This climactic sign, recorded only in John, drew crowds toward Jerusalem and hardened the authorities' resolve to put Jesus to death.
