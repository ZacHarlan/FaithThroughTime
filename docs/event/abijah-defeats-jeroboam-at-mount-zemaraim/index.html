Abijah Defeats Jeroboam at Mount Zemaraim

957 BC

Abijah of Judah calls out to Jeroboam and his army from Mount Zemaraim, reminding Israel of the Lord's covenant with David. Trusting in God, Judah wins a great victory and takes Bethel and nearby towns.
