Destruction of Sodom and Gomorrah

1897 BC

Fire and sulfur destroy Sodom and Gomorrah after the outcry against their wickedness reaches heaven, though angels lead Lot's family out; his wife looks back and becomes a pillar of salt. The doomed cities of the Dead Sea plain become Scripture's enduring emblem of judgment, invoked by prophets and by Jesus.
