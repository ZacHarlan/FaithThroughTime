Samuel Judges Israel at Mizpah

1096 BC

After twenty years of the ark at Kirjath-jearim, Samuel calls Israel to put away their idols and gathers them at Mizpah to fast and confess. When the Philistines attack, the LORD thunders against them and Israel routs them; Samuel sets up the stone Ebenezer, "Hitherto hath the LORD helped us."
