Fall of Jericho

1451 BC

Israel circles Jericho for seven days behind the ark until the walls collapse at the trumpet blast and shout, giving Joshua his first victory in Canaan; only faithful Rahab's family is spared. Famous excavations by John Garstang and Kathleen Kenyon made Jericho a landmark case in biblical archaeology.
