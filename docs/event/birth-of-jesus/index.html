Birth of Jesus

c. 5 BC

In Bethlehem, Mary gives birth to Jesus, announced by angels to shepherds and honored by magi from the east; Matthew and Luke present him as Messiah, son of David, and Immanuel. The Gospels root his birth in Micah's prophecy of a ruler from Bethlehem, opening the fulfillment of Israel's long story.
