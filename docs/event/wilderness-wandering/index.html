Wilderness Wandering

1491–1451 BC

After refusing to enter Canaan at the spies' fearful report, Israel wanders the wilderness for forty years until the unbelieving generation passes away. Sustained by manna, water from the rock, and the guiding cloud, the nation is disciplined and shaped for covenant life in the promised land.
