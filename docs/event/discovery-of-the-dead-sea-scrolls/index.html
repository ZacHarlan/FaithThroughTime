Discovery of the Dead Sea Scrolls

AD 1947

A Bedouin shepherd's find in a cave near Qumran in 1946-47 opened one of the greatest manuscript finds of modern times: some 900 scrolls from eleven caves, usually linked to a Jewish community at Qumran, often identified as Essene. Every Hebrew Bible book except Esther is represented, and the Great Isaiah Scroll predates the earlier known Hebrew manuscripts by about a thousand years.
