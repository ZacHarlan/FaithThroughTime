John Chrysostom Preaches at Constantinople

AD 398–404

Appointed archbishop of Constantinople in 398, John gains the name Chrysostom (golden-mouthed) for preaching that presses wealth, justice and care for the poor. His criticism of the court brings him exile in 403 and a final banishment in 404. He died in exile in 407.
