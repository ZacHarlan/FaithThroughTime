The Exodus

1491 BC

After ten plagues break Pharaoh's resistance, Israel departs Egypt under Moses on Passover night, freed from slavery to become a covenant nation. The Merneptah Stele, an Egyptian victory monument, preserves the earliest known extrabiblical mention of Israel as a people.
