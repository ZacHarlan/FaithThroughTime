Covenant of Circumcision

1897 BC

God gives Abraham circumcision as the enduring sign of the covenant, marking every male of his household and renaming Abram and Sarai as heirs of the promise of nations and kings. Though circumcision was practiced in Egypt and elsewhere, Israel uniquely made it a covenant emblem binding each generation to God.
