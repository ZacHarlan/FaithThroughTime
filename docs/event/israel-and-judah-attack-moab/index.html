Israel and Judah Attack Moab

c. 896 BC

Jehoram of Israel, Jehoshaphat and the king of Edom march against Moab after Mesha stops paying tribute. With no water for the army, Elisha promises a miraculous flood, and the allies drive Moab back to Kir-hareseth.
