## Session — v2 rebuild

**Goal:** Rebuild data and frontend; bring tests, harness and docs up to date (branch rebuild/v2).

**What changed**
- Data: SeedData.sql regenerated after an audit. 375 people, 241 events, 95 locations, 536 scripture references, 18 periods,
  66 books, 5 journeys. Explicit stable ids, one transaction with deferred foreign keys. One chronology (Ussher for the OT,
  standard dates for foreign rulers and church history). People renamed to be unique ("Mary, mother of Jesus", "John the
  Apostle", "James, brother of Jesus", ...). "Father of Lazarus" deleted. Brothers of Jesus have no father_id/mother_id
  (traditions differ) and are linked to Jesus by `sibling` rows. Duplicate parent/child relationship rows removed.
  Controlled vocabularies for people.role and events.category.
- Backend: GET /api/dataset and /data/dataset.json (Data/DatasetBuilder.cs, Models/DatasetDtos.cs,
  Endpoints/DatasetEndpoints.cs), `--export-dataset <path>` mode. Legacy endpoints kept.

  per view on design tokens (base.css), views home/timeline/tour/meet/family/map/read/about, search palette, share cards,
  detail sheet, tours and discoveries as JSON. D3 and Leaflet vendored.

  father_id/mother_id). Added GetLineage_BrotherOfJesus_HasJesusAsSibling.
